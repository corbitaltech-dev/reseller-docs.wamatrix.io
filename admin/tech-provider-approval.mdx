Tenants connect their WhatsApp Business Account through Meta's **Embedded Signup** flow, which runs on your Meta app. For that flow to work for the public, Meta must approve your business as a **Tech Provider** on the WhatsApp Business Platform.

Once approved, you can:

- Onboard businesses (your tenants) onto the WhatsApp Business Platform
- Send and receive messages on behalf of your tenants
- Manage message templates and phone numbers through the WhatsApp Business API

<Info>
  Until your app is approved, Embedded Signup only works for people who hold a role on the app (admins, developers, testers). Complete this guide before you enable **Embedded Signup** in [Settings → Channels](/admin/channels#whatsapp).
</Info>

## Before you start

Make sure you have the following:

| Requirement                   | Details                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Registered business**       | A legally registered business entity in your country.                                                          |
| **Live domain**               | The domain your platform runs on, with a public **Privacy Policy** page.                                       |
| **Meta Business Portfolio**   | Also called Business Manager. You need **admin** access to it.                                                 |
| **Business verification**     | Company documents (registration certificate, utility bill, or similar) for Meta's business verification.       |
| **Two screen recordings**     | Short videos of sending a message and creating a message template. See [Step 10](#10-record-videos-for-app-review). |

## Choose how to onboard

Meta offers two ways to become a Tech Provider:

| Option                              | When to choose it                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Independent Tech Provider**       | You manage tenants yourself. This is the usual choice for a reseller platform.                            |
| **Working with a Solution Partner** | You partner with a Meta Solution Partner. You need their **App ID** to create a partner solution.        |

Both options require App Review.

## Set up your Meta app

### 1. Create a Business Portfolio

- Go to [Meta Business Suite](https://business.facebook.com) and sign in with your Facebook account.
- This creates a **Business Portfolio**, the container for your WhatsApp assets.
- If you already have one, skip this step.

### 2. Register as a Meta developer

- Go to [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com) and click **Get Started**.
- Complete the registration. This turns your account into a **Developer Account**.

### 3. Create a Meta app

- Go to [Meta Apps](https://developers.facebook.com/apps) and click **Create App**.
- Select the **Business** app type.
- Choose your **Business Portfolio** when asked which portfolio to connect.

![The Create an app dialog, asking which business portfolio to connect to the app](/images/admin/tech-provider-create-app-portfolio.png)

- Enter the **app name** and **contact email**, confirm the Business Portfolio, and click **Create app**.

![The Create an app details step, with the app name, contact email and business portfolio fields](/images/admin/tech-provider-create-app-details.png)

<Note>
  The same app can also be used for [Messenger](/admin/channels#messenger). You do not need a separate app per channel.
</Note>

### 4. Add basic app info

In **App Dashboard → App settings → Basic**, add:

- **App icon**
- **Privacy Policy URL**
- **Category**
- **Platform** (Website, with your platform URL)

Meta checks these during App Review.

### 5. Add the WhatsApp product

- In the App Dashboard, open **Add Products**.
- Find **WhatsApp** and click **Set up**.

![The Add products to your app screen, with the WhatsApp Set up button](/images/admin/tech-provider-add-whatsapp-product.png)

- Continue until you reach the **Quickstart** panel.

![The WhatsApp Quickstart panel, with the Become a Tech Provider card under Scale your business](/images/admin/tech-provider-quickstart.png)

### 6. Start Tech Provider onboarding

- In the Quickstart panel, under **Scale your business**, find **Become a Tech Provider** and click **Start onboarding**.

![The Become a Tech Provider card, with the Start onboarding button](/images/admin/tech-provider-start-onboarding.png)

- Choose **Independent Tech Provider** or **Working with a Solution Partner**.
- Accept the **Tech Provider Terms** and click **Start onboarding**.

![The Onboard as a Tech Provider dialog, with the Independent Tech Provider and Working with a Solution Partner options](/images/admin/tech-provider-onboarding-type.png)

The **Tech Provider Onboarding** page opens. It has three sections: **Business Verification**, **App Review**, and **Access Verification**. The remaining steps complete each section.

## Complete Tech Provider onboarding

### 7. Verify your business

- Under **1. Business Verification**, click **Start verification**.

![The Business Verification section, with the Start verification button](/images/admin/tech-provider-business-verification.png)

- Submit your business details and legal documents.
- Verification can take a few days. Track the status in your [Business Info settings](https://business.facebook.com/settings/info).

<Note>
  Only an **admin** of the Business Portfolio can complete business verification.
</Note>

### 8. Create a partner solution

Only needed if you chose **Working with a Solution Partner** in [Step 6](#6-start-tech-provider-onboarding). Independent Tech Providers skip this step.

- Under **2. App Review**, click **Create partner solution**.

![The Create a partner solution card in the App Review section](/images/admin/tech-provider-partner-solution.png)

- Enter a **solution name** and your partner's **App ID**, then click **Send request**.

![The Create a partner solution dialog, with the solution name and partner App ID fields](/images/admin/tech-provider-partner-solution-form.png)

The solution shows as **Pending** until your partner accepts the request. You can continue with the next steps meanwhile.

### 9. Review app settings

- Under **2. App Review**, click **Review app settings**.

![The Review your app settings card in the App Review section](/images/admin/tech-provider-review-app-settings.png)

- Confirm the app icon, Privacy Policy URL, and category from [Step 4](#4-add-basic-app-info) are in place.

### 10. Record videos for App Review

Meta requires video evidence for the two permissions your app needs:

![The Record video documentation card, listing the videos required for each permission](/images/admin/tech-provider-record-videos.png)

| Permission                     | What the video must show                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `whatsapp_business_messaging`  | A message sent from your platform, and the same message arriving in the WhatsApp app (web or mobile).                    |
| `whatsapp_business_management` | A message template being created from your platform.                                                                     |

Record each video separately as a screen recording. Keep them short and make sure your platform's name and domain are visible.

For reference, see these [sample videos](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1PNfnyoWoBrMVEFygU4TF8XITBIqdtQ3R) that show what Meta expects for each permission.

<Info>
  Connect a test WhatsApp number to a tenant account first so you can record these flows from the tenant dashboard.
</Info>

### 11. Submit for App Review

- In the Quickstart panel, click **Begin App Review**. The request lists `whatsapp_business_messaging` and `whatsapp_business_management` as pending.

![The App Review Requests page, with a draft request for the two WhatsApp permissions](/images/admin/tech-provider-app-review-requests.png)

- For each permission, explain how your platform uses it and attach the matching video from [Step 10](#10-record-videos-for-app-review).
- In **App verification details**, confirm the **Website** platform and site URL, and describe how a reviewer can test your integration.

![The App verification details dialog, with the Website platform and the reviewer instructions field](/images/admin/tech-provider-app-verification-details.png)

- Submit the request. Meta notifies you by email and in **Developer Alerts**.

<Warning>
  Do not provide your personal Facebook credentials in the reviewer instructions. Keep the explanation short and business-focused, and avoid technical jargon.
</Warning>

### 12. Complete access verification

- Under **3. Access Verification**, click **Start verification**.

![The Access Verification section, with the Start verification button](/images/admin/tech-provider-access-verification.png)

- Provide the requested business information to confirm you are eligible as a Tech Provider.

### 13. Confirm your status

When all three sections are approved, the Tech Provider Onboarding page shows **Congratulations! You are now a Tech Provider**.

![The Tech Provider Onboarding page, with all verification steps approved](/images/admin/tech-provider-approved.png)

<Info>
  Review timelines vary. Business verification and App Review can each take from a few days to a few weeks. Start well before your planned launch.
</Info>

## After approval

Finish the app configuration that Embedded Signup relies on:

1. In **Facebook Login for Business → Configurations**, create a configuration with the **Embedded Signup** login variation and the `whatsapp_business_management` and `whatsapp_business_messaging` permissions. Do **not** include `catalog_management`.
2. In **Facebook Login for Business → Settings**, add your platform domain to **Allowed Domains for the JavaScript SDK**. The tenant connection page is served from your domain.
3. In [**Settings → Channels → WhatsApp**](/admin/channels#meta-app-credentials), enter the **App ID**, **App Secret**, and the **Embedded Signup Config ID (no catalog)**, then save.
4. Click [**Connect Webhook**](/admin/channels#webhook-configuration) to register the webhook with Meta.
5. Turn on **Embedded Signup (no catalog)** so tenants can start connecting.
