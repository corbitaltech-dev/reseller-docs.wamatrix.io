## Before You Start

<Warning>
  Set up [**registration, email**](#registration--verification-settings),
  [**bot protection**](#bot-protection), and [**social login**](#social-login)
  before tenants start registering. Changes to these settings apply only to
  new registrations and do not affect existing tenants.
</Warning>

## Registration & Verification Settings

Go to **Settings → Tenant Settings**.

![The Tenant Settings page, with the registration toggles and the tenant plan limit alert](/images/admin/tenant-settings.png)

### Allow Tenant Registration

- **Enable Registration** allows tenants to create their own accounts. This
  setting is **enabled by default**.

When **Enable Registration** is turned off, tenants cannot create new
accounts. The registration page shows **Registration closed** instead of
the sign-up form.

Tenants who have already registered can continue using their accounts
normally.

### Verification and Emails

Each of these is **off by default**.

- **Enable Email Verification** — Sends a verification email after the tenant registers. The email contains a **Verify Email Address** button. The tenant must click the button before signing in. The link is valid for 60 minutes and can be sent again.

* **Enable Registration Success Email** — Sends a short email confirming that the tenant's registration was received. It is sent as soon as the account is created.

* **Enable Send Welcome Mail** — Sends a welcome email when the tenant's account is ready. The email includes their plan and a link to their dashboard. If email verification is enabled, the welcome email is sent after the tenant verifies their email.

<Note>
  Only tenants who register themselves need to verify their email. [**Tenants
  added from the panel**](#from-the-panel) and tenants who sign up with
  Google or Facebook are already verified.

  Email verification works only after SMTP is configured. No verification
  email is sent before SMTP is set up.
</Note>

## Tenant Plan Limit Alerts

**Tenant Near Plan Limit** sends an email when a tenant is close to reaching
a plan limit. It is **off by default**.

The percentage field sets when the email is sent. It is **80%** by default
and can be set from **50%** to **99%**.

<Note>
  **Tenant Near Plan Limit** sends the email to the tenant when they are
  close to reaching a limit on their plan.

  If the reseller wants to receive an email when their own plan is close to
  its limit, enable **Plan Limit Almost Reached** under **Settings → Admin
  Notifications**.

  These are separate settings.
</Note>

## Bot Protection

Go to **Settings → Re-Captcha** to protect login, forgot-password, and tenant registration from bots.

![The Bot Protection page, with the reCAPTCHA and Turnstile provider options and their key fields](/images/admin/bot-protection.png)

The platform supports:

- **Google reCAPTCHA v3**
- **Cloudflare Turnstile**

<br />

Only one provider can be active at a time. Bot protection is **off by default**.

The selected provider requires its **Site Key** and **Secret Key**.

<Warning>
  Incorrect keys can prevent tenants from logging in, not just from
  registering. Make sure the keys match the selected provider before enabling
  bot protection.
</Warning>

## Social Login

Go to **Settings → Social Auth** to let tenants and reseller staff sign in with **Google** or **Facebook**.

![The Social Login Settings page, with the Google and Facebook credential and redirect URI fields](/images/admin/social-login.png)

Both providers are **off by default** and require:

- **Google** — Client ID and Client Secret
- **Facebook** — App ID and App Secret

Each provider shows a **redirect URL** to paste into its developer console:

- **Google** — Authorized Redirect URI
- **Facebook** — Valid OAuth Redirect URI

<br />

A new tenant signing in with Google or Facebook for the first time gets an
account created automatically.

<Info>
  Copy the redirect URL exactly as shown, including `https://`. If it does not
  match, social login will not work.
</Info>

## Adding a tenant

### From the Panel

![The Add Tenant form, with the tenant, workspace, plan, and password fields](/images/admin/add-tenant-form.png)

1. Go to **Tenants** and click **Add Tenant**.
2. Fill in:
    - **Full name** and **Email** — The tenant's name and email address.
    - **Workspace** — The tenant's business or company name.
    - **Tenant Name** — Used as the tenant's subdomain. It is filled in
      automatically from the **Workspace** name using lowercase letters and
      numbers. It can be changed if needed.
    - **Phone** — The tenant's phone number. **Country** and **Address** can
      also be added.
    - **Plan** — Optional. Leave **Select a plan** if the plan will be
      assigned later.
    - **Password** and **Confirm Password** — Set and confirm the tenant's
      password.
3. Click **Save**.

<Note>
  If no plan is selected when creating a tenant, the tenant's account is
  created without a subscription. The tenant can sign in, but all
  plan-based limits are set to 0, so they cannot use the platform normally.

  Assign a plan from the tenant's **Edit** form to give the tenant access to
  the plan's features and limits.
</Note>

The tenant's password is set directly when the account is created. No password setup email is sent. The reseller must share the login details with the tenant separately.

### Public Registration

When **Enable Registration** is turned on, tenants can create their own accounts and choose a plan during registration. Their workspace is created automatically after registration, so no manual setup is needed from the panel.

<Note>
  New tenant registrations stop when the reseller reaches the tenant limit
  on their own plan.

  The tenant sees **"Registration is currently unavailable"** and cannot
  create an account. No separate notification is sent to the reseller.

  Check the current tenant usage under [**Billing → My
  Subscription**](/admin/plan-limits-and-customer-access#tenant-usage).
</Note>

## What the Tenant Sees

After a tenant signs in, they are taken to their workspace dashboard. The
features and limits available to them depend on the plan assigned to their
account.

<br />

If a feature is not included in their plan, the feature still appears in
the sidebar with a lock icon. When the tenant clicks it, a message explains
that the feature is not available on their current plan and shows an option
to **Upgrade Plan**.

<br />

To check what a tenant sees, open the tenant from **Tenant List** and
select **Impersonate**. The tenant's workspace opens using their account,
so the reseller can check the same pages and features available to that
tenant.

<br />

While impersonating, a **"You are impersonating this tenant"** banner
appears at the top. The reseller cannot access the admin panel until
impersonation is stopped.

<br />

Select **Stop Impersonating** in the banner to return to the reseller
account.
