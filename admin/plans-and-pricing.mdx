Each plan includes:

- **Price** — The amount charged to the tenant.
- **Billing period** — How often the tenant is charged.
- **Feature limits** — Limits for features such as contacts, WhatsApp channels, and AI features.

A tenant can be subscribed to **one plan at a time**.

## Before You Start

<Warning>
  A base currency must be configured before a plan can be created. See
  [Currency setup](/admin/currency-setup). The **Add Plan** button stays disabled,
  with a tooltip explaining why, until a currency exists.
</Warning>

<Info>
  A tenant plan cannot have higher limits than your own plan. If your plan
  allows 10,000 contacts, a tenant plan can allow up to 10,000 contacts,
  but not more.

  Each field shows the maximum allowed value, such as **Max 10000**.
  **Unlimited** is available only when your own plan has an unlimited limit.
</Info>

## Create a Plan

![The Add Plan form: plan details and pricing on the left, plan features with their limits on the right](/images/admin/plan-form.png)

1. Go to **Plans** and click **Add Plan**.
2. Fill in the plan details on the left:
    - **Plan name** and **Description**.
    - **Slug** — auto-generated from the name, and locked once saved.
    - **Billing period** and **Price** — one period only (Monthly,
      Quarterly, Half-Yearly, Yearly, or Lifetime).
    - **Private** — hides the plan from the public pricing page, The plan can only be assigned manually to tenants.
    - **Free plan** — see [Free Plans](#free-plans) below.
3. On the right, in **Plan features**, turn on every feature and limit the
   plan should include.
4. Click **Save**.

<Info>
  The **Save button** stays disabled until all required features limits are set correctly. An error message shows which required limit is missing or incorrect.

  - **Channels**, **contacts**, **conversations** and **cloud media library
    space** — each needs a value above zero.
  - **Channels** also needs at least one connection method, such as manual
    connect or Embedded Signup.
  - **API access**, **appointment forms** and **AI chat assist** each need
    their matching quota turned on.
</Info>

## Free Plans

When **Free plan** is enabled, the plan does not require a price or billing
period. Instead, you set **Trial days** from 1 to 365 days. Tenants can sign
up and use the platform without paying.

<Note>
  A tenant can use a free plan only if they have never used a paid plan or
  another free plan before.

  For example, if a tenant has already used a paid plan, they cannot switch
  to a free plan later. A tenant who has already used a free plan also
  cannot use another free plan.
</Note>

While any tenant is still on the trial, the **Free plan** switch is
disabled and the plan cannot be made paid. It unlocks once every trial on
that plan has ended.

## Editing and Removing Plans

- **Duplicate** — Creates a copy of the plan. The copy starts as private and
  must be published separately.
- **Make private / Make public** — Changes whether the plan is visible on the
  storefront. Pricing and features remain unchanged.
- **Delete** — Permanently deletes the plan.
