Branding controls the platform logo, colours, email appearance, and sender identity.

Branding has three settings areas:

- **System** — Configure the platform name, general system settings, and logos.
- **Email Layout** — Configure the appearance and layout of outgoing emails.
- **Email** — Configure the sender identity and SMTP connection used to send emails.
- **Legal** — Link to the externally hosted Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions pages.

These settings are optional and can be configured at any time.

<Note>

  SMTP must be configured for the platform to send standard tenant emails, including registration, welcome, and notification emails.
</Note>

## System Core Settings

Go to **Settings → System**.

The System page contains the main platform settings and logo configuration:

![The System Core Settings page, with the site name, locale defaults, and the logo and favicon upload fields](/images/admin/system-core-settings.png)

- **Site Name** — The platform name shown throughout the panel. Required.
- **Site Description** — A short description of the platform.
- **Timezone** — The default timezone for dates and times.
- **Date Format** — How dates are displayed.
- **Time Format** — How times are displayed.
- **Default Country Code** — The country code applied by default.
- **Site Logo** — The primary logo. Recommended **200 × 50 px**, maximum **2 MB**.
- **Dark Logo** — The logo used in dark mode. Recommended **200 × 50 px**, maximum **2 MB**.
- **Favicon** — The browser tab icon. Recommended **32 × 32 px**, maximum **512 KB**.

The platform accepts **JPEG, PNG, and WebP** files for logos. **ICO** files are also supported for the Favicon.

An existing file can be selected from the [**Media Library**](/admin/media-library) by clicking **Add Media** instead of uploading a new file.

<Warning>
  SVG files are not supported for the Site Logo, Dark Logo, or Favicon because these images are rendered directly on the platform's domain.
</Warning>

## Email Layout

Go to **Settings → Email Layout**.

Email Layout controls the visual appearance applied to outgoing email templates. The settings are divided into three tabs:

![The Email Layout page on its Branding tab, with the live email preview alongside](/images/admin/email-layout.png)

- **Branding** — The logo shown in the email header. This is separate from the Site Logo; if it is left empty, the Site Logo is used instead.
- **Colours** — The header, content, footer, button, and link colours. Pick each one from the colour picker or enter a hex value.
- **Footer** — The text at the bottom of every email. Supports the `{date_year}` and `{company_name}` placeholders.

Click **Save Changes** on the final tab to save the Email Layout configuration.

## Sender Identity (SMTP)

Go to **Settings → Email**.

Turn on the **Enable Email** switch to activate email sending.

Enter the required SMTP details:

![The Email Configuration page, with the SMTP connection fields, sender identity, and the Send Test Email box](/images/admin/smtp-settings.png)

- **SMTP Host**
- **Port**
- **Username**
- **Password**
- **Encryption**

Configure the **Sender Name** and **Sender Email** to determine the sender information displayed to tenants.

Click **Test** to send a test email and verify that the SMTP configuration is working correctly before relying on it for tenant emails.

<Warning>
  Email delivery is disabled until SMTP is configured. No emails will be sent without a valid SMTP connection.
</Warning>

## Legal

Go to **Settings → Legal**.

Legal supplies the **Privacy Policy** and **Terms & Conditions** links shown to tenants. The pages are hosted externally — the workspace administrator publishes them and enters the two URLs here.

![The Legal settings page, with the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions link fields](/images/admin/legal-settings.png)

Turn on the switch in the top right of the card, then enter:

- **Privacy Policy Link** — The full URL, including `https://`.
- **Terms & Conditions Link** — The full URL, including `https://`.

Both are required once the switch is on. Click **Save Changes** to save.

### Where the links appear

On the sign-up page, tenants must tick a box that says _"I agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy."_

![The tenant sign-up page, with Terms of Service and Privacy Policy shown as links beside the agreement checkbox](/images/admin/signup-consent-links.png)

- **Switch on** — the two names are clickable and open your pages.
- **Switch off** — the same wording shows as plain text, with nothing to click.

Either way, tenants still have to tick the box to sign up.
