## 1. Set Up Your White-Label Brand

- In [**System Settings**](/admin/branding#system-core-settings), configure your **company name, logo, favicon, and other branding options**.
- Configure [**Email**](/admin/branding#sender-identity-smtp) and [**Email Layout**](/admin/branding#email-layout) to customize your tenant emails.
- Under [**Legal**](/admin/branding#legal), add links to your **Terms and Privacy Policy** pages. These links are displayed during **tenant registration**.

## 2. Set Up Your Currency

- Open [**Setup → Currency**](/admin/currency-setup#set-the-base-currency) and enter the **Currency Name, ISO Code, and Symbol**.
- Make sure you enter correct currency details before creating plans. The [**ISO Code cannot be changed after an active subscription**](/admin/currency-setup#what-becomes-fixed-once-tenants-exist).

## 3. Create Your Plans

- Open [**Plans**](/admin/plans-and-pricing#create-a-plan) and create the plans you want to offer to your tenants.
- Set the [**plan name, price, billing period, features, and limits**](/admin/plans-and-pricing#before-you-start) for each plan.

## 4. Set Up Billing & Payments

- In [**Payment Gateway Settings**](/admin/billing-and-payouts#payment-gateway-settings), add the required details for the **payment gateways** you want to enable for your tenants.
- Under [**System Settings → Company Information**](/admin/billing-and-payouts#company-information), add your **company details** that will be displayed on invoices.
- In [**Invoice Settings**](/admin/billing-and-payouts#invoice-settings), set the **invoice prefix, starting invoice number, and footer notes**, and choose whether **tax should be applied to invoices**.
- If tax is enabled, configure the required **tax name, rate, type, currency, and description** under [**Taxes**](/admin/billing-and-payouts#taxes).

## 5. Onboard Your First Tenant

- In [**Tenant Settings**](/admin/onboarding-a-sub-tenant#registration-verification-settings), configure **tenant registration, email verification, welcome emails, and registration success emails**.
- Under [**reCAPTCHA Settings**](/admin/onboarding-a-sub-tenant#bot-protection), configure **Google reCAPTCHA or Cloudflare Turnstile** to protect the tenant registration form.
- In [**Social Login**](/admin/onboarding-a-sub-tenant#social-login), configure **Google or Facebook login** if you want to allow tenants to register or log in with their social accounts.
- Open [**Tenants**](/admin/onboarding-a-sub-tenant#create-a-tenant) and create your first tenant account, then assign a **plan**.
