## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply & get approved">
    Submit your application at wamatrix.io/reseller. Every application is reviewed personally — expect a reply within 72 hours and a founder onboarding call if accepted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your custom domain">
    Point your subdomain (e.g. app.youragency.com) to our platform with a single CNAME record. SSL is provisioned automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create client workspaces">
    Spin up a dedicated workspace for each client in seconds. Each workspace is isolated — its own WhatsApp number, contacts, campaigns, and team inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your pricing and go live">
    Define your own plans and pricing, assign them to workspaces, and share your branded URL with clients. You set the margin; we never touch your client relationships.
  </Step>
</Steps>
