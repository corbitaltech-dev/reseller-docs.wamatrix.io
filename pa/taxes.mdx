Automatically add GST, VAT, or other taxes to tenant invoices. Instead of adding the tax manually to each invoice, set up the tax rate once, and WaMatrix.io applies it automatically.

## What you see on the screen

Click **Setup → Taxes** in the sidebar menu to open this page.

The list shows every tax rate you've created:

- **Name** — what the tenant sees on the invoice, such as GST or VAT. Click it to edit the tax.
- **Rate** — the percentage charged, for example `18.00`.
- **Type** — always **Percentage**. See [Percentage is the only type](#percentage-is-the-only-type) below.
- **Currency** — the currency this rate applies to.
- **Active** — a switch that turns the tax active or inactive, without deleting it.

<Note>
  An info banner at the top of this page reminds you that a tax rate only charges tenants once tax is set to **Active**. See [Tax on invoices](#tax-on-invoices) below.
</Note>

## Before you start

<Warning>
  You cannot add a tax until you set your reseller's base currency under [**Currency setup**](/pa/currency-setup). The **Add Tax** button and form stay disabled, with a message telling you to set your base currency first — a tax needs a currency to know which invoices it applies to.
</Warning>

## Adding a tax

Click **Add Tax** to create a new tax rate.

<Frame caption="The Add Tax panel, with the name, rate, type, currency, and description fields">
  <img src="/images/pa/add-tax.png" alt="The Add Tax panel, with the name, rate, type, currency, and description fields" />
</Frame>

| Field | Description |
| ----- | ----------- |
| **Name** | What the tenant sees on the invoice, such as `GST` or `VAT`. Up to 255 characters. |
| **Rate** | The percentage to charge, from 0 to 100. You can use two decimal places, for example `18.50`. |
| **Type** | **Percentage** is the only option. See [below](#percentage-is-the-only-type). |
| **Currency** | Your reseller's base currency. Set one in [Currency setup](/pa/currency-setup) first, or this list is empty. |
| **Description** | An optional note for your own reference. Tenants never see it. Up to 500 characters. |

### Percentage is the only type

The **Type** field only offers **Percentage**. There is no fixed-amount tax (a flat charge like "$5 tax" instead of a rate) on this screen — every tax you add is a percentage of the invoice.

<Note>
  Once saved, make sure [tax is set to **Active**](#tax-on-invoices), or the rate is never charged.
</Note>

## You can add more than one tax

You can add multiple tax rates. For example, if you charge both a state tax and a national tax, add each tax separately using the same currency, then enable both.

Every **active** tax rate in an invoice's currency is charged on that invoice, all at once:

- On most invoices, each tax you've added shows as its own line, for example one line for `GST (18.00%)` and another for `CESS (2.00%)`.
- On some invoices — such as a bank transfer request a tenant submits for approval — multiple taxes are combined into a single line, with the names joined by `+`, for example `GST (18.00%) + CESS (2.00%)`.

<Note>
  A tax's name on the invoice always shows the rate next to it in brackets, like `GST (18.00%)`. It never shows as plain text like "GST 18%".
</Note>

There is no way to say "only charge this tax, not that one" for a particular tenant or plan. A tax rate applies the same way to every tenant invoice in that currency, once it is active.

## How tax is calculated

Tax is never charged on the full invoice amount straight away. It is worked out in this order:

1. Start with the invoice **subtotal** (the plan price).
2. Subtract any [coupon discount](/pa/coupons) the tenant used.
3. On an upgrade invoice, subtract any [proration credit](/pa/subscriptions#upgrades-and-downgrades) for time left on the tenant's old plan.
4. Charge each active tax rate as a percentage of what is left.

<Warning>
  Tax is calculated after discounts and proration credits are applied. This means the tax amount is based on the final amount the tenant pays, not the original plan price.

  For example, if a large proration credit reduces the upgrade amount, the Tax amount is reduced as well. See [Invoices](/pa/invoices#customer) for how this appears in the pricing breakdown.
</Warning>

## Tax on invoices

You can control whether tax is added to new invoices from [**Billing & payouts → Invoice Settings**](/pa/billing-and-payouts#invoice-settings).

- **Active** — tax is added to new invoices using your active tax rates.
- **Inactive** — tax is not added to new invoices. Your tax rates remain saved and can be used again when you activate tax.

<Note>
  This setting controls tax for invoices. The tax rates you configure on this page determine how much tax is charged.
</Note>

## Editing and deleting a tax

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit** | Change the tax's name, rate, type, currency, or description. |
| **Active switch** | Turn the tax on or off without deleting it. |
| **Delete** | Remove the tax from your list. |

<Warning>
  Changing a tax's rate, or deleting it, only affects invoices made **after** the change. Invoices already issued keep the exact tax they were charged at the time — editing or deleting a tax rate never rewrites a tenant's past invoice.
</Warning>

<Note>
  A deleted tax can still be looked up by any old invoice that used it — deleting only stops it from being charged on **new** invoices. This is different from coupon codes, which cannot be reused once deleted; a tax's old invoice history is simply kept as a record.
</Note>

### Bank transfer invoices freeze slightly earlier

A tenant paying by [bank transfer](/pa/offline-payments) sees the tax amount worked out when they **submit** their payment proof, not when you approve it. If you rename a tax or change its rate between the tenant's submission and your approval:

- The **amount** charged never changes — it stays exactly what the tenant was quoted at submission.
- The **name** printed on the final invoice is looked up fresh at approval, so it can read slightly differently from what the tenant originally saw, even though the amount is the same.


## This does not affect your own WaMatrix.io subscription

Taxes you add here apply only to invoices you send to your own tenants. They have nothing to do with what you pay WaMatrix.io for your own reseller subscription — that is billed on whatsmark.io itself, not from inside this panel.
