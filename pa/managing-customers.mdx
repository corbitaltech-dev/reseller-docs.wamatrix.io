After a tenant is onboarded, you can manage their workspace from the **Tenant List**. You can view tenant details, check their workspace, update their status, and delete their account.

## What you see on the screen

Go to **Tenant → Tenant List** from the sidebar.

<Note>
  To create a tenant workspace, see [Onboarding a tenant](/pa/onboarding-a-sub-tenant).
</Note>

<Frame caption="The Tenant List, with the status summary cards above it and the account counter beside Add Tenant">
  <img src="/images/pa/tenant-list.png" alt="The Tenant List, with the status summary cards above it and the account counter beside Add Tenant" />
</Frame>

### Tenant summary

The cards at the top show the number of tenants in each status:

| Card | What it shows |
| ---- | ------------- |
| **Total Tenants** | Total number of tenant workspaces. |
| **Active** | Tenants who can access the platform. |
| **Suspended** | Tenants whose access is temporarily blocked. |
| **Inactive** | Tenants whose access has been deactivated. |
| **Pending Payment** | Tenants waiting for an offline payment request to be approved. |

<Tip>
  The **Tenants** counter next to **Add Tenant** shows the number of tenant accounts in use and the account limit, such as **5 / 6**.
</Tip>

### Tenant list


| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Workspace** | Tenant's workspace or business name. |
| **Plan** | Tenant's current plan. |
| **Email** | Workspace owner's email address. |
| **Phone** | Workspace owner's phone number. |
| **Status** | Tenant's current status. |
| **Sub. Ends** | End date of the current subscription period. |
| **Actions** | Actions available for the tenant. |

### Tenant actions

Open the **Actions** menu at the end of a tenant row.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **View** | Opens the [tenant's detail page](#tenant-detail-page). |
| **Edit** | Changes the tenant's details. |
| **Impersonate** | Opens the [tenant's workspace](#impersonating-a-tenant) as the workspace owner. |
| **Delete** | Opens the [tenant deletion dialog](#delete-a-tenant). |

## Tenant detail page

Click **View** to open the tenant's detail page.

<Frame caption="A tenant's detail page, with the profile, subscription, activity and invoices panels">
  <img src="/images/pa/tenant-detail.png" alt="A tenant's detail page, with the profile, subscription, activity and invoices panels" />
</Frame>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Profile" icon="user">
    Shows the tenant's subdomain, owner email, phone number, join date, current plan, billing cycle, and account status. Email and phone numbers show **Verified** or **Not Verified**.

    Available actions are **Edit**, **Impersonate**, **Suspend**, and **Deactivate**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Current Subscription" icon="repeat">
    Shows the tenant's plan, plan description, billing cycle, and subscription start and end dates.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Recent Activity" icon="history">
    Shows the tenant's recent activity. Use **All**, **Impersonated**, or **Updated** to filter the list.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Recent Invoices" icon="receipt">
    Shows the tenant's recent successful invoices with their status, date, amount, and download option. Select **Payment History** to view the full payment history, including failed payment attempts.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Impersonating a tenant

Click **Impersonate** to open the tenant's workspace as the workspace owner. This can be used to check the tenant's account or troubleshoot an issue.

An amber banner shows **You are impersonating this tenant** while impersonation is active.

<Frame caption="The tenant's workspace while impersonating, with the amber banner and Stop Impersonating button">
  <img src="/images/pa/tenant-impersonate.png" alt="The tenant's workspace while impersonating, with the amber banner and Stop Impersonating button" />
</Frame>

<Tip>
  Click **Stop Impersonating** in the banner to return to your reseller account.
</Tip>

## Suspend, deactivate, and reactivate

You can change tenant access from the tenant's detail page.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Suspend** | Temporarily blocks tenant access. The plan and billing remain unchanged. |
| **Deactivate** | Blocks tenant access and changes the status to **Inactive**. |
| **Activate** | Restores access for a suspended or inactive tenant. The plan remains unchanged. |

<Info>
  Tenants with **Pending Payment** status cannot be suspended or deactivated. Review the offline payment request under **Offline Payments** first.
</Info>

## Delete a tenant

Click **Delete** from the tenant's row to open the deletion dialog.

<Warning>
  Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. It removes the tenant's users, contacts, chat history, campaigns, connected channels, media, and billing history. Connected WhatsApp channels are also disconnected.
</Warning>

If the tenant has a active subscription, you can choose:

| Option | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Delete after subscription ends** | The tenant keeps access until the subscription ends. The tenant and all related data are then deleted. |
| **Instant delete** | Deletes the tenant and all related data immediately. |

<Note>
  If the tenant has no subscription, only **Instant delete** is available.
</Note>

### Scheduled deletions

You can cancel a scheduled deletion before the subscription ends by selecting **Cancel scheduled deletion** from the tenant's row.

While deletion is scheduled:

- The tenant keeps full access.
- No data is deleted.
- The tenant receives email reminders **7 days and 1 day** before the subscription ends.

**Instant delete** does not send reminders because the tenant is deleted immediately.
