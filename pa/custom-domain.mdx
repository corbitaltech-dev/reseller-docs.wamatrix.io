## What you need

- **A subdomain**, such as `app.yourcompany.com`. Your main domain, `yourcompany.com`, cannot be used for this setup because it does not support the required CNAME record.
- **Access to your DNS provider** — Cloudflare, GoDaddy, Route 53, or where your domain is managed.
- **One CNAME record.** You only need to add one record. No TXT record, no email to confirm.

## Where to find it

Once you have paid for your Reseller plan, go to [**app.wamatrix.io**](https://app.wamatrix.io). Enter your email on the **Find your workspace** screen and click **Continue**, then sign in with your password.

In your workspace, open **Settings → Custom Domain**.

## Connect your domain

<Frame caption="The Custom Domain tab, with the empty Domain name field and the Connect Domain button">
  <img src="/images/pa/custom-domain-empty.png" alt="The Custom Domain tab, with the empty Domain name field and the Connect Domain button" />
</Frame>

Type the domain you want to use, such as `app.yourcompany.com`, then click **Connect Domain**.

| Rule | What happens if you break it |
| --- | --- |
| Enter the domain only | Don't include `https://` or a path. Enter only `app.yourcompany.com`. |
| Not a WaMatrix.io address | You cannot use the platform's own domain or a subdomain under it. |
| Not already taken | If another workspace is already using the domain, you cannot add it. |

The domain is saved and marked **Not verified**. A **DNS Setup Instructions** card opens below it, showing the exact CNAME record to create.

<Frame caption="The domain marked Not verified, with the three DNS Setup Instructions steps and the CNAME target underneath">
  <img src="/images/pa/custom-domain-dns.png" alt="app.acmecorp.com marked Not verified with a Verify button, above DNS Setup Instructions showing the CNAME record with its Value / Target of cname.partners.wmio.test" />
</Frame>

## Add the CNAME record

Open your DNS provider and add a **CNAME** record using the values shown on the screen:

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Type | `CNAME` |
| Name / Host | Your subdomain, such as `app` |
| Value / Target | Use the **Copy** button next to it — the target is specific to your workspace. |

The finished record in Cloudflare:

<Frame caption="A finished CNAME record in Cloudflare: Name app, Target cname.partners.wamatrix.io, Proxy status DNS only">
  <img src="/images/pa/custom-domain-cloudflare-record.png" alt="Cloudflare's Add record dialog with Type CNAME, Name app, Target cname.partners.wamatrix.io, and the Proxy status toggle switched off showing DNS only" />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  Leave **Proxy status** off, as shown above. If you turn it on, the SSL certificate can never be issued and the domain will never verify.
</Warning>

## Verify it

Once you save the record, keep the page open. We automatically check your domain every minute, so you don't need to verify it manually. Most domains are ready in one to five minutes.

The status beside your domain shows where your setup has reached:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Not verified** | Waiting for your DNS record. |
| **Pending** | Record found. The SSL certificate is being issued — nothing for you to do. |
| **Failed** | Something is wrong. The line underneath says what. |
| **Verified** | Done. Your panel is live on your own address. |

The page stops checking after five minutes and shows **Automatic checks paused**. Click **Verify** whenever you want to check again.

<Frame caption="The Custom Domain tab after verification, with the domain marked Verified and Primary, and a delete icon beside it">
  <img src="/images/pa/custom-domain-verified.png" alt="The Custom Domain tab after verification, with the domain marked Verified and Primary, and a delete icon beside it" />
</Frame>

## Change to a different domain

Use the delete icon beside the domain, confirm, then connect the new one.

<Warning>
  Your current panel address will stop working while you switch domains. You and your tenants cannot open it until the new domain is verified.

  Your billing pages on **app.wamatrix.io** keep working, so you can still sign in there and manage your account.

  To keep the gap short, add the new domain's CNAME record at your DNS provider **before** you remove the old one.
</Warning>
