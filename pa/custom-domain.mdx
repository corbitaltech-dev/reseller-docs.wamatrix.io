## What you need

- **Your domain**, such as `yourcompany.com`, or a subdomain, such as `app.yourcompany.com`.
- **Access to your DNS provider** — Cloudflare, GoDaddy, Route 53, or where your domain is managed.
- **One CNAME record.** You only need to add one record. No TXT record, no email to confirm.

## Where to find it

Once you have paid for your Reseller plan, go to [**app.wamatrix.io**](https://app.wamatrix.io). Enter your email on the **Find your workspace** screen and click **Continue**, then sign in with your password.

In your workspace, open **Settings → Custom Domain**.

## Connect your domain

<Frame caption="The Custom Domain tab, with the empty Domain name field and the Connect Domain button">
  <img src="/images/pa/custom-domain-empty.png" alt="The Custom Domain tab, with the empty Domain name field and the Connect Domain button" />
</Frame>

Type the domain you want to use, such as `app.yourcompany.com`, then click **Connect Domain**.

| Rule | What happens if you break it |
| --- | --- |
| Enter the domain only | Don't include `https://` or a path. Enter only `app.yourcompany.com`. |
| Not a WaMatrix.io domain | You cannot use the platform's own domain or a subdomain under it. |

The domain is saved and marked **Not verified**. A **DNS Setup Instructions** card opens below it, showing the exact CNAME record to create.

<Frame caption="The domain marked Not verified, with the three DNS Setup Instructions steps and the CNAME target underneath">
  <img src="/images/pa/custom-domain-dns.png" alt="app.acmecorp.com marked Not verified with a Verify button, above DNS Setup Instructions showing the CNAME record with its Value / Target of cname.partners.wmio.test" />
</Frame>

## Add the CNAME record

Open your DNS provider and add a **CNAME** record using the values shown on the screen:

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Type | `CNAME` |
| Name / Host | Your subdomain, such as `app` |
| Value / Target | Use the **Copy** button next to it — the target is specific to your workspace. |

The finished record in Cloudflare:

<Frame caption="A finished CNAME record in Cloudflare: Name app, Target cname.partners.wamatrix.io, Proxy status DNS only">
  <img src="/images/pa/custom-domain-cloudflare-record.png" alt="Cloudflare's Add record dialog with Type CNAME, Name app, Target cname.partners.wamatrix.io, and the Proxy status toggle switched off showing DNS only" />
</Frame>

<Warning>
  Leave **Proxy status** off, as shown above. If you turn it on, the SSL certificate can never be issued and the domain will never verify.
</Warning>

## Verify it

Once you save the record, keep the page open. We automatically check your domain every minute, so you don't need to verify it manually. Most domains are ready in one to five minutes.

The status beside your domain shows where your setup has reached:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Not verified** | Waiting for your DNS record. |
| **Pending** | Record found. The SSL certificate is being issued — nothing for you to do. |
| **Failed** | Something is wrong. The line underneath says what. |
| **Verified** | Done. Your reseller workspace is live on your own domain. |

The page stops checking after five minutes and shows **Automatic checks paused**. Click **Verify** whenever you want to check again.

## Change to a different domain

To move your reseller workspace to a new domain, use the **Change** button beside your current domain.

<Tip>
  **Add the new CNAME record first.** Add the new domain's CNAME record at your DNS provider **before** you change the domain. Then the new domain can verify quickly, and your reseller workspace stops working for less time.
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Change">
    Click **Change** next to your domain. You see your current domain and what stops working when you change it. Read this list before you go on.

    <Frame caption="Change custom domain, with the current domain app.acmecorp.com, the list of what will stop working, the New domain name field, and the I understand tick box">
      <img src="/images/pa/custom-domain-change.png" width="420" style={{ margin: '0 auto' }} alt="Change custom domain, with the current domain app.acmecorp.com, the list of what will stop working, the New domain name field, and the I understand tick box" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the new domain">
    Type the new domain in **New domain name**, such as `app.yournewbrand.com`. Don't include `https://`. It must be different from the domain you have now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Tick the box that starts **I understand changing this domain**, then click **Change domain**. The button stays off until you tick the box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the new domain">
    The new domain is saved and marked **Not verified**. Add its CNAME record if you haven't yet, and wait for it to [verify](#verify-it).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Your old domain stops working the moment you click Change domain.** Your reseller workspace and every tenant workspace on it stop working until the new domain is verified.

  Nothing moves to the new domain automatically. After it verifies, update every place that still uses the old domain: your WhatsApp, Messenger, and Instagram webhooks (where Meta sends your messages), your payment gateways, your store connections, and any links you have shared.
</Warning>

You can still log in at **app.wamatrix.io** to see your plan, billing, and invoices while the new domain is being verified.

### Remove the domain

- Click the delete icon next to your domain. This removes it without adding a new one.
- You see the same list and the same tick box as when you change a domain.
- Your reseller workspace stops working until you connect and verify a new domain.
- If you want to move to a new domain, use **Change** instead.
