You have a reseller panel and nothing set up in it yet. Before you can onboard a tenant and they can pay for a plan, five things need to be ready.

## The five steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your white-label brand" icon="palette">
    Put your own name, logo, and email address on the panel. Do this first, because tenants see it the moment they land on your signup page.

    <CardGroup cols={3}>
      <Card title="Branding" icon="image" href="/pa/branding#system-core-settings">
        Company name, logo, and favicon.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Email" icon="mail" href="/pa/branding#sender-identity-smtp">
        The address your tenant emails are sent from, and [how those emails look](/pa/branding#email-layout).
      </Card>
      <Card title="Legal" icon="scale" href="/pa/branding#legal">
        Your Terms and Privacy Policy links, shown when a tenant signs up.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set your currency" icon="coins">
    Your platform collects payment in **one** currency only. Every tenant pays in it, wherever they are, and every plan and invoice uses it.

    Open [**Setup → Currency**](/pa/currency-setup#set-the-base-currency) and enter the **Currency Name**, **ISO Code**, and **Symbol**.

    <Warning>
      Double-check the **ISO Code** before you save. Once a tenant has a paid subscription, it [locks](/pa/currency-setup#what-you-can-and-cannot-change-later). You cannot switch from INR to USD later.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your plans" icon="layers">
    A plan is what a tenant subscribes to. It decides the amount they pay for the plan, and what they get in that plan:

    - **Features** — which features are turned on for them.
    - **Limits** — how much they can use, such as how many contacts, channels, and conversations.

    Open [**Plans**](/pa/plans-and-pricing#create-a-plan) and set the [**name, price, billing period, features, and limits**](/pa/plans-and-pricing#before-you-start) for each one.

    <Note>
      Your own WaMatrix.io plan sets the maximum. If your plan allows 10,000 contacts, a tenant plan can allow up to 10,000, but never more. [To offer more, upgrade your own subscription](/pa/plans-and-pricing#you-cannot-offer-more-than-your-own-plan-allows).
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up billing" icon="wallet">
    A tenant cannot subscribe until you turn on at least one payment method. The money goes straight to your own bank or gateway account, not to WaMatrix.io.

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Payment gateways" icon="credit-card" href="/pa/billing-and-payouts#payment-gateway-settings">
        Turn on the ways tenants can pay you, online or by bank transfer.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Company information" icon="building" href="/pa/billing-and-payouts#company-information">
        Your business name and address, printed on every invoice.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Invoice settings" icon="receipt" href="/pa/billing-and-payouts#invoice-settings">
        Invoice prefix, starting number, and footer notes.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Taxes" icon="percent" href="/pa/taxes">
        Add a tax rate, if you charge tax.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open signups and add your first tenant" icon="user-plus">
    This is the last step. Set up the registration page your tenants will land on, add bot protection to stop fake signups, turn on Google or Facebook login if you want it, then add your first tenant and give them a plan.

    <CardGroup cols={2}>
      <Card title="Registration settings" icon="settings" href="/pa/onboarding-a-sub-tenant#registration-verification-settings">
        Turn signups on or off, and set the emails a new tenant receives.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Bot protection" icon="shield" href="/pa/onboarding-a-sub-tenant#bot-protection">
        Stop fake signups with Google reCAPTCHA or Cloudflare Turnstile.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Social login" icon="key-round" href="/pa/onboarding-a-sub-tenant#social-login">
        Let tenants sign in with Google or Facebook.
      </Card>
      <Card title="Create a tenant" icon="user-plus" href="/pa/onboarding-a-sub-tenant#adding-a-tenant">
        Open **Tenants**, add an account, and give it a plan.
      </Card>
    </CardGroup>
  </Step>
</Steps>
