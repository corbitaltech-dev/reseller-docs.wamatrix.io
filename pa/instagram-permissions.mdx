Your tenants connect their Instagram account through your Meta app. Before that works for everyone, Meta must check your app and approve the **permissions** it asks for. A permission is one thing your app is allowed to do, like reading or sending Instagram messages.

This page shows which permissions to ask for, how to set up your Meta app, and how to send it to Meta for approval.

<Info>
  Until Meta approves your app, only people you add to your app (admins, developers and testers) can connect an Instagram account. Use this time to test everything before you send the app to Meta.
</Info>

## Before you start

| You need | Why |
| -------- | --- |
| **A Meta app** | Use your Messenger app for **Connect via Facebook Page**. To also offer **Connect via Instagram Login**, you need a second app. See [Two ways to connect](#two-ways-to-connect). |
| **A verified Business Portfolio** | Meta checks that your business is real before it approves some permissions. See [Tech Provider Approval](/pa/tech-provider-approval#complete-tech-provider-onboarding). |
| **A test Instagram account** | It must be a **Business** or **Creator** account, not a personal one. To test **Connect via Facebook Page**, link it to a Facebook Page. |
| **A second Instagram account** | You use it to send test messages to your test account. |

## Two ways to connect

Tenants can connect Instagram in two ways. Each way needs its own permissions. Set up only the ways you want to offer.

| Connect method | Use it when | Setup in Meta |
| -------------- | ----------- | ------------- |
| **Connect via Facebook Page** | The tenant's Instagram account is linked to a Facebook Page. | **API setup with Facebook login** |
| **Connect via Instagram Login** | The tenant's Instagram account has no Facebook Page. | **API setup with Instagram login** |

<Note>
  Meta lets each app use only one of these two setups. To offer both connect methods, use two Meta apps:

  - **Connect via Facebook Page** — your Messenger app. Its **App ID** and **App Secret** go in the platform's **App ID** and **App Secret** fields.
  - **Connect via Instagram Login** — a second app. Its **Instagram app ID** and **Instagram app secret** go in **Instagram App ID** and **Instagram App Secret**.

  Meta explains this rule in [Customize the Instagram use case](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/development/create-an-app/instagram-use-case).
</Note>

## Permissions you need

Type the permission names exactly as shown. Meta uses these exact names on its screens.

### Connect via Facebook Page

Ask for these eight permissions.

| Permission | What it lets the platform do |
| ---------- | ---------------------------- |
| `instagram_basic` | Show the account's name, username and photo. |
| `instagram_manage_messages` | Read and send Instagram messages. |
| `instagram_manage_comments` | Read and reply to comments on posts, and to posts where the account is tagged. |
| `pages_show_list` | Show the tenant a list of their Facebook Pages, so they can pick one. |
| `pages_manage_metadata` | Link the Page to your app, so new messages reach the platform. |
| `pages_messaging` | Send messages through the Page. |
| `pages_read_engagement` | Read the Page's name and details. |
| `business_management` | Connect Pages that belong to a Business Portfolio. Meta approves this one only after your business is verified. |

<Note>
  Meta also adds `public_profile` by itself. You do not need to pick it.
</Note>

### Connect via Instagram Login

Ask for these three permissions.

| Permission | What it lets the platform do |
| ---------- | ---------------------------- |
| `instagram_business_basic` | Show the account's name, username and photo. |
| `instagram_business_manage_messages` | Read and send Instagram messages. |
| `instagram_business_manage_comments` | Read and reply to comments on posts, and to posts where the account is tagged. |

### Also ask for this feature

A **feature** is a special approval from Meta. It is not a permission, but you ask for it on the same Meta screen.

| Feature | What it does |
| ------- | ------------ |
| **Business Asset User Profile Access** | Shows the real name and photo of people who message a tenant's Facebook Page. Ask for it if you also use this app for [Messenger](/pa/messenger-permissions). |

## Set up your Meta app

Meta's App Dashboard is built around **use cases**. A use case is one job your app does, like managing Instagram messages. When you add a use case, Meta adds the permissions and setup pages for it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the Instagram use case" icon="plus">
    **To add it to an app you already have** (for example, your Messenger app):

    1. Open your app in [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/apps).
    2. In the left menu, click **Use cases**.
    3. Add **Manage messaging & content on Instagram**.

    **To create a new app:**

    1. In [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/apps), click **Create App**.
    2. Enter the app name and contact email. Click **Next**.
    3. Under **Use cases**, pick **Manage messaging & content on Instagram**. Click **Next**.
    4. Pick your verified **Business Portfolio**. Click **Next**.
    5. Check the **Requirements** and the **Overview**, then click **Go to dashboard**.

    <Note>
      Meta shows only the use cases that can work together in one app. If **Manage messaging & content on Instagram** is not in the list, create a new app for it. You cannot remove a use case after you add it.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Instagram setup page" icon="settings">
    In the left menu, click **Use cases**. Next to **Manage messaging & content on Instagram**, click **Customize**.

    The menu on this page shows **Permissions and features**, **API setup with Instagram login**, **API setup with Facebook login** and **Webhooks**. You use these in the next steps.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up Connect via Facebook Page" icon="facebook">
    Skip this step if you only offer **Connect via Instagram Login**. Do this step in your Messenger app.

    1. Click **API setup with Facebook login**, then click **Add required messaging permissions**.
    2. Click **Permissions and features**. Click **Add** next to `instagram_manage_comments`.
    3. Check that the app also has the **Engage with customers on Messenger from Meta** use case. It gives you `pages_messaging` and `pages_manage_metadata`. The Instagram use case does not have these two. If you followed [Messenger Permissions](/pa/messenger-permissions), your app already has it.
    4. In the left menu, go to **Facebook Login for Business → Configurations** and click **Create configuration**.
    5. Enter a name, like `Instagram Page Connect`. On the **Choose access token** screen, pick **User access token**.

    <Frame caption="The Create a Configuration screen in Meta, on the Choose access token step, with User access token selected">
      <img src="/images/pa/instagram-meta-access-token.png" alt="The Create a Configuration screen in Meta, on the Choose access token step, with User access token selected" />
    </Frame>

    6. On the **Permissions** screen, add the eight permissions from [Connect via Facebook Page](#connect-via-facebook-page). Click **Create**.
    7. Meta shows a **Configuration ID**. Copy it.
    8. In the platform, go to **Settings → System Settings → Channels → Instagram**. Paste the ID in **Facebook Login for Business Config ID**. Enter this app's **App ID** and **App Secret**. You find them in Meta under **App settings → Basic**.
    9. On the same page, copy the **OAuth Redirect URI** from the **Connect via Facebook Page** card. This is the page Meta sends the tenant back to after they log in.
    10. Back in your Meta app, go to **Facebook Login for Business → Settings**. Paste it in **Valid OAuth Redirect URIs** and save.

    <Frame caption="Client OAuth settings in Meta, with the Valid OAuth Redirect URIs field marked">
      <img src="/images/pa/instagram-meta-redirect-uris.png" alt="Client OAuth settings in Meta, with the Valid OAuth Redirect URIs field marked" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up Connect via Instagram Login" icon="log-in">
    Skip this step if you only offer **Connect via Facebook Page**. If you offer both methods, do this step in your second app.

    1. Click **API setup with Instagram login**, then click **Add required messaging permissions**. Check that the list shows the three permissions from [Connect via Instagram Login](#connect-via-instagram-login).
    2. Copy the **Instagram app ID**. Click **Show** and copy the **Instagram app secret**.

    <Frame caption="The Customize use case page in Meta, on API setup with Instagram login, with the Instagram app ID and Instagram app secret marked">
      <img src="/images/pa/instagram-meta-app-id-secret.png" alt="The Customize use case page in Meta, on API setup with Instagram login, with the Instagram app ID and Instagram app secret marked" />
    </Frame>

    3. In the platform, go to **Settings → System Settings → Channels → Instagram**. Paste them in **Instagram App ID** and **Instagram App Secret**.
    4. The platform also needs an **App ID** and **App Secret**. If you offer only Instagram Login, enter this app's. You find them in Meta under **App settings → Basic**.
    5. Copy the **OAuth Redirect URI** from the **Connect via Instagram Login** card.
    6. Back in your Meta app, on the same page, find **Set up Instagram business login** and click **Business login settings**. Paste it in **OAuth redirect URIs** and click **Save**.

    <Frame caption="The Business login settings window in Meta, with the OAuth redirect URIs field">
      <img src="/images/pa/instagram-meta-business-login.png" alt="The Business login settings window in Meta, with the OAuth redirect URIs field" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect the webhook" icon="webhook">
    A **webhook** is the link Meta uses to send new messages and comments to the platform.

    1. In the platform, go to [**Settings → System Settings → Channels → Instagram**](/pa/channels#instagram). Turn on the connect methods you want and click **Save Changes**.
    2. Copy the **Callback URL** and **Verify Token**.
    3. In your Meta app, paste them in the place for each connect method you offer:

    | Connect method | Where to paste them in Meta |
    | -------------- | --------------------------- |
    | **Connect via Facebook Page** | In the use case menu, click **Webhooks**. Pick **Instagram** as the product. |
    | **Connect via Instagram Login** | **API setup with Instagram login → Configure webhooks**. |

    4. Click **Verify and save**. Then turn on the events. [Instagram webhook](/pa/channels#instagram-webhook) shows which events to turn on.

    <Frame caption="Configure webhooks in Meta, with the Callback URL, Verify token, and webhook fields such as comments and messages set to Subscribed">
      <img src="/images/pa/instagram-meta-webhooks.png" alt="Configure webhooks in Meta, with the Callback URL, Verify token, and webhook fields such as comments and messages set to Subscribed" />
    </Frame>

    <Note>
      Meta sends webhooks only to a published app. You publish your app in the last step of [Send your app to Meta for approval](#send-your-app-to-meta-for-approval).
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test with your own account" icon="flask-conical">
    1. In your Meta app, open **App roles → Roles** and add yourself as an **Admin** or **Tester**.
    2. For Instagram Login, also add your test Instagram account. On **API setup with Instagram login**, under **Generate access tokens**, click **Add account** and log in.
    3. Log in to a tenant account on your platform and connect your test Instagram account.
    4. From your second Instagram account, send a message to the test account. Reply to it from the platform inbox.
    5. In the left menu of your Meta app, open **Testing**. It shows if any permission still needs a test before App Review.

    <Tip>
      Record your screen while you test. Meta asks for this video when you send your app for approval.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Send your app to Meta for approval

Meta calls this step **App Review**. When Meta approves a permission, your app gets **Advanced Access** to it. That means it works for all your tenants, not just your test accounts.

If you use two Meta apps, do these steps for each app.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tell Meta you serve other businesses">
    On your app's **Dashboard**, click **Become a Tech Provider**, then **Yes, I'm a Tech Provider**. Meta then lists what your app still needs before you can publish it. See [Tech Provider Approval](/pa/tech-provider-approval) for business verification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the permissions to send">
    - **Connect via Instagram Login** — On **API setup with Instagram login**, open **Complete app review** and click **Go to app review**. Meta shows a list of permissions. Keep only the three from [Connect via Instagram Login](#connect-via-instagram-login). Untick the others, like `instagram_business_content_publish` and `instagram_business_manage_insights`. The platform does not use them.
    - **Connect via Facebook Page** — Open **Permissions and features**. For each of the eight permissions, click **Actions** and pick **Add to App Review**. Also add the **Business Asset User Profile Access** feature if you use this app for Messenger.

    Meta may also add the **Human Agent** feature by itself. The platform does not use it for Instagram. Remove it if Meta lets you. Meta rejects permissions and features that your app does not use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your request">
    In the left menu, go to **Review → App Review** and click **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your app settings">
    Click **Review your app settings**. Add your app icon (1024 × 1024 pixels), Privacy Policy URL, app category and business email. You cannot go on until these are done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give Meta a test login">
    Meta's reviewer tests your app by hand. Click **Provide reviewer instructions**. Give them a login to a tenant account on your platform, and short steps to repeat your test.

    <Warning>
      If the reviewer cannot log in and test, Meta rejects the whole request. Never give your personal Facebook or Instagram login.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explain each permission">
    Click each permission in the request. For each one:

    - Write one or two simple sentences on how your tenants use it. For example: "Tenants read and reply to their Instagram messages from our inbox."
    - Upload your test video.
    - Tick the box to agree to Meta's rules for that permission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit for Review** at the bottom right. If your business is not verified yet, finish that first under **Review → Verification**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish your app">
    After Meta approves your app, click **Publish** in the left menu. Check the list, then click the **Publish** button at the bottom right. Meta sends Instagram messages to the platform only after your app is published.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  These rules come from Meta, not WaMatrix.io. Approval can take from a few days to a few weeks. Meta tells you the result by email and in your app's **Alerts**. Meta's [App Review for Instagram API](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/app-review) page has the full details.
</Note>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My tenant's Facebook Page does not show up when they connect. Why?" icon="search-x">
    The Page probably belongs to a Business Portfolio. Your app needs `business_management` approved for those Pages to show up. Check that it is in your Facebook Login for Business configuration, and that Meta has approved it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Comments do not reach the platform. Why?" icon="message-square-off">
    Check three things. Meta has approved your comments permission. You turned on the `comments`, `live_comments` and `mentions` events in your Meta app. Your app is published.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need a new Meta app for Instagram?" icon="layers">
    Only if you offer both connect methods. Meta lets each app use one Instagram setup. Add **Connect via Facebook Page** to your Messenger app, and use a second app for **Connect via Instagram Login**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My Meta app shows Add Product, not Use cases. What do I do?" icon="package-plus">
    Your app was made with Meta's older app types. Click **Add Product**, find **Instagram** and click **Set up**. You then find **API setup with Instagram login** and **API setup with Facebook login** under **Instagram** in the left menu.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
