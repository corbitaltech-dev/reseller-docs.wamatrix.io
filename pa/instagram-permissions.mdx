Tenants connect their Instagram account through your Meta app. Meta must approve your app's Instagram permissions before tenants outside your team can connect.

<Info>
  Until Meta approves your app, only people with a role on your app (admins, developers, testers) can connect an account. Use this to test before you send your app for review.
</Info>

## Before you start

| You need | Details |
| -------- | ------- |
| **A Meta app** | The same app you use for WhatsApp or Messenger works. |
| **A verified Business Portfolio** | Meta asks for Business Verification before it approves the permissions. See [Tech Provider Approval](/pa/tech-provider-approval#complete-tech-provider-onboarding). |
| **A test Instagram account** | An Instagram professional account (Business or Creator). To test **Connect via Facebook Page**, link it to a Facebook Page. |
| **A second Instagram account** | To send test messages to your test account. |

## Required permissions

The permissions depend on how tenants connect.

### Connect via Facebook Page

| Permission | What the platform uses it for |
| ---------- | ----------------------------- |
| `public_profile` | Reads the basic profile of the person who logs in. |
| `instagram_basic` | Reads the account's name, username, and photo. |
| `instagram_manage_messages` | Sends and receives Instagram messages. |
| `instagram_manage_comments` | Reads and replies to comments and mentions. |
| `pages_show_list` | Lists the tenant's Facebook Pages so they can pick one. |
| `pages_manage_metadata` | Links the Page to your app so messages arrive. |
| `pages_messaging` | Sends messages through the Page. |
| `pages_read_engagement` | Reads the Page details. |

### Connect via Instagram Login

| Permission | What the platform uses it for |
| ---------- | ----------------------------- |
| `instagram_business_basic` | Reads the account's name, username, and photo. |
| `instagram_business_manage_messages` | Sends and receives Instagram messages. |
| `instagram_business_manage_comments` | Reads and replies to comments and mentions. |

## Set up your Meta app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the Instagram product" icon="plus">
    In your Meta app, open **Add Product**. Find **Instagram** and click **Set up**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up Connect via Facebook Page" icon="facebook">
    Skip this step if you only use Instagram Login.

    In your Meta app, go to **Facebook Login for Business → Configurations** and click **Create configuration**. On **Choose access token**, select **User access token**.

    <Frame caption="The Create a Configuration screen in Meta, on the Choose access token step, with User access token selected">
      <img src="/images/pa/instagram-meta-access-token.png" alt="The Create a Configuration screen in Meta, on the Choose access token step, with User access token selected" />
    </Frame>

    On **Permissions**, add the eight permissions from [Connect via Facebook Page](#connect-via-facebook-page). Save the configuration and copy its **Configuration ID**.

    In the platform, go to **Settings → Channels → Instagram**. Paste the ID in the **Facebook Login for Business Config ID** field. On the same page, copy the **OAuth Redirect URI**.

    Back in your Meta app, go to **Facebook Login for Business → Settings**. Paste the address in **Valid OAuth Redirect URIs** and save.

    <Frame caption="Client OAuth settings in Meta, with the Valid OAuth Redirect URIs field marked">
      <img src="/images/pa/instagram-meta-redirect-uris.png" alt="Client OAuth settings in Meta, with the Valid OAuth Redirect URIs field marked" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up Connect via Instagram Login" icon="log-in">
    Skip this step if you only use the Facebook Page way.

    In your Meta app, go to **Instagram → API setup with Instagram login**. Copy the **Instagram app ID** and the **Instagram app secret**. Click **Show** to see the secret.

    <Frame caption="API setup with Instagram login in Meta, with the Instagram app ID and Instagram app secret marked">
      <img src="/images/pa/instagram-meta-app-id-secret.png" alt="API setup with Instagram login in Meta, with the Instagram app ID and Instagram app secret marked" />
    </Frame>

    In the platform, go to **Settings → Channels → Instagram**. Paste them in the **Instagram App ID** and **Instagram App Secret** fields. On the same page, copy the **OAuth Redirect URI**.

    Back in your Meta app, on the same page, click **Business login settings**. Paste the address in **OAuth redirect URIs** and click **Save**.

    <Frame caption="The Business login settings window in Meta, with the OAuth redirect URIs field">
      <img src="/images/pa/instagram-meta-business-login.png" alt="The Business login settings window in Meta, with the OAuth redirect URIs field" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save and set up the webhook" icon="webhook">
    In the platform, go to [**Settings → Channels → Instagram**](/pa/channels#instagram). Enter the **App ID** and **App Secret**, turn on your connect methods, and click **Save Changes**.

    Then copy the **Callback URL** and **Verify Token** from the platform into your Meta app. See [Instagram webhook](/pa/channels#instagram-webhook) for the two places to paste them.

    <Frame caption="Configure webhooks in Meta, with the Callback URL, Verify token, and webhook fields such as comments and messages set to Subscribed">
      <img src="/images/pa/instagram-meta-webhooks.png" alt="Configure webhooks in Meta, with the Callback URL, Verify token, and webhook fields such as comments and messages set to Subscribed" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test with your own account" icon="flask-conical">
    1. In your Meta app, open **App roles** and add your account as an **Admin** or **Tester**. For Instagram Login, also give your Instagram account the **Instagram Tester** role.
    2. Log in to a tenant account on your platform and connect your test Instagram account.
    3. Send a message to it from your second Instagram account. Reply from the platform inbox.

    <Tip>
      Record this test. You can use the video for App Review.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Submit for App Review

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask for Advanced Access">
    Go to **App Review → Permissions and Features**. Ask for **Advanced Access** to each permission in the list for your connect method.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explain each permission">
    For each permission, write one or two sentences on how tenants use it. Add your test video.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give the reviewer a test login">
    Give Meta's reviewer a tenant login on your platform and simple steps to repeat your test.

    <Warning>
      Meta rejects the whole request if the reviewer cannot log in and test. Do not give your personal Facebook or Instagram login.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your app Live">
    After approval, switch your Meta app to **Live**. Meta sends Instagram messages only while the app is Live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  These rules come from Meta, not WaMatrix.io. Review can take from a few days to a few weeks.
</Note>
