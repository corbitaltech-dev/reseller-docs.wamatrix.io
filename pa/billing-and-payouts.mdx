A tenant cannot buy a plan until you turn on a payment method. Set that up here, along with the business details on your invoices and any tax you charge.

The money goes straight to you. Online payments arrive in your own gateway account, and bank transfers in your own bank account.

## Payment Gateway Settings

Payment Gateway Settings is where you choose the payment methods your tenants can use to pay for their plans.

<Frame caption="Payment Gateway Settings page showing the available payment methods">
  <img src="/images/pa/payment-gateways.png" alt="Payment Gateway Settings page showing the available payment methods" />
</Frame>

| Payment method | Type | How it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bank Transfer** | Manual | The tenant transfers to your bank account and uploads proof. You approve it before their plan starts. |
| **Razorpay** | Instant | Card, UPI, netbanking, or wallet. |
| **PayPal** | Instant | The tenant pays with their PayPal balance or card. |
| **Tap Payments** | Instant | Card payments. |
| **Paymob** | Instant | Card and wallet payments. |

<Tip>
  Turning on **Bank Transfer** alongside an online gateway is a safe starting point. A tenant who cannot pay online still has a way through.
</Tip>

### Configure a payment gateway

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the gateway">
    In **Payment Gateway Settings**, select the one you want to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Use the keys from your own account with that provider. This is what makes the money arrive with you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable and save">
    Turn the gateway on and save. It now appears to tenants at checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the connection">
    Click **Test Connection**. It is better to find a wrong key now than when a tenant is trying to pay.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Offline Payment Settings

If you turn on **Bank Transfer**, your tenants need to know where to pay the money. Open **Settings → Payment Settings → Offline Payment** and fill in your bank details.

Your tenants see these details and pay into this account, so check every digit before you save. A wrong account number is a payment that never arrives.

<Frame caption="The Offline Payment Settings page, with the Bank & UPI Details card on the left and the Activation SLA set to 2 business days">
  <img src="/images/pa/offline-payment-settings.png" alt="The Offline Payment Settings page, with the Bank & UPI Details card on the left and the Activation SLA set to 2 business days" />
</Frame>

| Field | Description |
| ----- | ----------- |
| **Bank Name** | The bank holding your account. |
| **Account Holder Name** | The name on the account. |
| **Account Number** | Your account number. |
| **IFSC Code** | The branch code for the transfer. |
| **UPI ID** | Your UPI address, if you accept UPI. |
| **Activation SLA (Business Days)** | How many working days you need to check a payment and activate the plan. Enter 1 to 30. Tenants are shown this number, so promise only what you can deliver. |

The tenant transfers the money, uploads their proof, and the request waits for you under [**Offline payments**](/pa/offline-payments).

## Company Information

Your business details appear on every invoice your tenants receive. Add them before you issue your first one.

<Frame caption="The Company Information settings page, with the business identity fields used on invoices">
  <img src="/images/pa/company-information.png" alt="The Company Information settings page, with the business identity fields used on invoices" />
</Frame>

Fill in **Company Name**, **Company Email**, **WhatsApp Number**, **GST Number**, **Country**, **State / Province**, **ZIP / Postal Code**, and **Company Address**, then click **Save Changes**.

## Invoice Settings

Invoice Settings controls how your invoices are numbered, the notes shown at the bottom, and whether tax is added.

<Frame caption="The Invoice Details settings page, with the invoice prefix, next invoice number, footer notes, and the tax toggle">
  <img src="/images/pa/invoice-settings.png" alt="The Invoice Details settings page, with the invoice prefix, next invoice number, footer notes, and the tax toggle" />
</Frame>

### Invoice Prefix

Set the prefix added to every invoice number. With a prefix of `INV-`, your invoices read `INV-0001`, `INV-0002`, and so on.

<Note>
  Changing the prefix affects **future invoices only**. Invoices you have already issued keep their original numbers.
</Note>

### Next Invoice Number

The number your next invoice will use. It goes up by one automatically after that. Set it to `1` to start from the beginning.

### Footer Notes

Text added to the bottom of every invoice. Most resellers use it for payment instructions, bank details, a thank-you line, or anything their accountant needs.

### Enable Tax on Invoices

Turn this on before you add a tax. While it is off, no tax is added to any invoice, even if you have already created one.

## Taxes

Add the tax rates that apply to your tenant invoices.

### Add a tax

<Frame caption="The Add Tax panel, with the name, rate, type, currency, and description fields">
  <img src="/images/pa/add-tax.png" alt="The Add Tax panel, with the name, rate, type, currency, and description fields" />
</Frame>

| Field | Description |
| ----- | ----------- |
| **Name** | What the tenant sees on the invoice, such as GST or VAT. Up to 255 characters. |
| **Rate** | The percentage to add, from 0 to 100. |
| **Type** | **Percentage** is the only option. |
| **Currency** | Your base currency. Set one in [Currency setup](/pa/currency-setup) first, or this list is empty. |
| **Description** | Optional note for your own reference. Up to 500 characters. |

Once saved, make sure [**Enable Tax on Invoices**](#enable-tax-on-invoices) is on, or the rate is never applied.

<Note>
  If you change a tax rate, it affects new invoices only. Invoices already issued keep their original rate.
</Note>

## How payments are settled

<Info>
  The platform does not hold your tenants' payments.
</Info>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Online gateways">
    **Razorpay**, **PayPal**, **Tap Payments**, and **Paymob** all run on credentials from your own account, so the money is transferred into your gateway account.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Bank Transfer">
    Tenants pay you directly using the bank details you provide, so the money arrives in your bank account. You then check their proof and approve or reject the request under [**Offline payments**](/pa/offline-payments).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
