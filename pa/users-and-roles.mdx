Anyone helping you manage the platform should have their own login, so you don't have to share your credentials.

You do this in two steps. First build a **role**, then add the person under **Users** and give them that role.

## Roles

Click **Setup → Role** in the sidebar. A role is a group of permissions, like view tenants but not billing. You build it once and give it to as many staff as you want.

### Create a role

Click **Add Role**, give it a **Role Name**, then tick the permissions that role should have.

<Frame caption="The Add Role panel, with the permission checkboxes for each area">
  <img src="/images/pa/add-role.png" alt="The Add Role panel, with the permission checkboxes for each area" />
</Frame>

You can set permissions for **Plans**, **Coupons**, **Taxes**, **Transactions**, **Activity Log**, **Notifications**, **Tenants**, **Tenant Billing**, **Currency**, **My Billing**, **Email Templates**, **Media Library**, **Seeder Library**, **Settings**, **Users**, and **Roles**.

Most areas offer **View**, **Create**, **Edit**, and **Delete**.

<Warning>
  You cannot delete a role while someone is using it. Move those staff to another role first, then delete it.
</Warning>

## Users

A user is one person on your team with their own email and password to sign in. Each one carries a role, which decides what they can open.

Click **Setup → User** in the sidebar to see everyone you have added. The list shows each person's name, phone, email, status, and the date they joined.

### Add a user

Click **Add User**. The form has two tabs: **User Details** and **Role**.

<Frame caption="The Add User panel, with the User Details tab and the Role tab beside it">
  <img src="/images/pa/add-user.png" alt="The Add User panel, with the User Details tab and the Role tab beside it" />
</Frame>

On **User Details**, enter who the person is and the credentials they sign in with.

| Field | Description |
| ----- | ----------- |
| **Name** | The person's name. |
| **Email** | The address they sign in with. It cannot already be in use, by your staff or by any tenant. |
| **Phone** | Their phone number. Required, and it must be different from your other staff. |
| **Password** | The password for their account. You set it here and pass it to them yourself. |

On **Role**, decide what they can do. Pick one of these two:

| Access type | What they get |
| --- | --- |
| **Role** | Only the permissions in the role you give them. Roles are set up under [Roles](#roles) above. |
| **Administrator Access** | Every permission in the panel, the same as your own access. No role needed. |

<Warning>
  **Administrator Access** cannot be limited by a role. Anyone who has it can open your billing, your tenants, and your other staff accounts, so give it only to a business partner or someone you trust with your own login.
</Warning>

### Edit a user

Open any staff member to change their name, email, phone, or role.

Two rows are locked: **your own account** and **the account owner**. Both are changed from **Profile** instead.
