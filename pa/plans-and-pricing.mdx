A tenant can be subscribed to **one plan at a time**. Each plan has three parts:

| Part | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Price** | The amount charged to the tenant. |
| **Billing period** | How often the tenant is charged. |
| **Feature limits** | Limits for contacts, WhatsApp channels, AI features, and more. |

## Before you start

<Warning>
  A base currency must be configured before a plan can be created. See [Currency setup](/pa/currency-setup). The **Add Plan** button stays disabled, with a tooltip explaining why, until a currency exists.
</Warning>

<Info>
  A tenant plan cannot have higher limits than your own plan. If your plan allows 10,000 contacts, a tenant plan can allow up to 10,000 contacts, but not more.

  Each field shows the maximum allowed value, such as **Max 10000**. **Unlimited** is available only when your own plan has an unlimited limit.
</Info>

## Create a plan

<Frame caption="The Add Plan form: plan details and pricing on the left, plan features with their limits on the right">
  <img src="/images/pa/plan-form.png" alt="The Add Plan form: plan details and pricing on the left, plan features with their limits on the right" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plan form">
    Go to **Plans** and click **Add Plan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the plan details">
    On the left side of the form:

    | Field | Description |
    | ----- | ----------- |
    | **Plan name** | The name shown to tenants. |
    | **Description** | A short summary of the plan. |
    | **Slug** | Auto-generated from the name, and locked once saved. |
    | **Billing period** | One period only — Monthly, Quarterly, Half-Yearly, Yearly, or Lifetime. |
    | **Price** | The amount charged for that period. |
    | **Private** | Hides the plan from the public pricing page. The plan can only be assigned manually to tenants. |
    | **Free plan** | See [Free plans](#free-plans) below. |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the plan features">
    On the right, in **Plan features**, turn on every feature and limit the plan should include.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Free plans

When **Free plan** is enabled, the plan does not require a price or billing period. Instead, you set **Trial days** from 1 to 365 days. Tenants can sign up and use the platform without paying.

<Note>
  A tenant can use a free plan only if they have never used a paid plan or another free plan before.
</Note>

While any tenant is still on the trial, the **Free plan** switch is disabled and the plan cannot be made paid. It unlocks once every trial on that plan has ended.

## Editing and removing plans

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Duplicate** | Creates a copy of the plan. The copy starts as private and must be published separately. |
| **Make private / public** | Changes whether the plan is visible on the storefront. Pricing and features remain unchanged. |
| **Delete** | Permanently deletes the plan. |
