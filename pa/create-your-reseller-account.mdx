Start your own WhatsApp business under your brand.

## Step 1: Choose a reseller plan

Open the [reseller pricing page](https://wamatrix.io/reseller). It shows the **Reseller** plan, its price, and everything it includes.

<Frame caption="The Reseller Pricing page, with Monthly, Quarterly and Yearly at the top left, INR and USD at the top right, the Client workspaces slider set to 5, and the Get Reseller button">
  <img src="/images/pa/reseller-pricing-plan.png" alt="The Reseller Pricing page, with Monthly, Quarterly and Yearly billing buttons, an INR and USD switch, the Reseller card with a Client workspaces slider from 5 to 500 set to 5, the monthly price, a list of included limits, the Get Reseller button, and an Everything in Reseller feature list on the right" />
</Frame>

Make three choices:

| Choice | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, or **Yearly** | How often you pay. |
| **INR** or **USD** | The currency you pay in. |
| **Client workspaces** | How many client workspaces you can have. Move the slider to choose. The price changes with it. |

Then click **Get Reseller** to open the sign-up form.

## Step 2: Create your reseller account

Fill in the sign-up form in two parts: **Account** and **Workspace**.

### Account

<Frame caption="The Account step, filled in with Rahul Mehta, rahul@acmedigital.in, a +91 WhatsApp number and INR — Indian Rupee, with the Reseller | Monthly label at the top right">
  <img src="/images/pa/reseller-signup-account.png" alt="The Account step of the Create your workspace form, filled in with Rahul Mehta, rahul@acmedigital.in, a +91 WhatsApp number and INR — Indian Rupee, with the Reseller | Monthly label at the top right" />
</Frame>

Make sure the top right of the form says **Reseller**.

<Tip>
  You may see **Sign up with Google**, **GitHub**, or **Facebook** above the form. Use one to sign up faster. It fills in your name and email for you, and you don't need to set a password.
</Tip>

Then fill in these fields:

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Full name** | Your name, such as "Rahul Mehta". |
| **Email address** | A valid email address. You use it to log in, and we send your verification email to it. |
| **Password** | At least 8 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number, and a symbol. |
| **Confirm password** | Type the same password again. |
| **WhatsApp number** | A valid WhatsApp number, with your country code. We send a verification code to it. |
| **Currency** | **INR** or **USD**. The currency you pay in. It comes from your choice on the pricing page. |

<Warning>
  **You cannot change this currency later.** All your bills from WaMatrix.io will be in this currency, so pick the one you want to pay in.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Use a new email and WhatsApp number. An email or number that is already used for another account will not work here.
</Note>

Click **Continue**.

### Workspace

<Frame caption="The Workspace step, with Workspace name Acme Digital, Workspace ID acmedigital marked as available, and both agreement boxes ticked">
  <img src="/images/pa/reseller-signup-workspace.png" alt="The Workspace step of the Create your workspace form, with Workspace name Acme Digital, Workspace ID acmedigital marked as available, both agreement boxes ticked, and the Create workspace button" />
</Frame>

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Workspace name** | Your business name, such as "Acme Digital". |
| **Workspace ID** | A short, unique name, such as `acmedigital`. Use lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens only. |

The form checks your **Workspace ID** as you type. Look for **"is available"** below it. If it is taken, try another one.

Tick both boxes to agree to the **Terms of Service**, **Privacy Policy**, and **Fair Use & Acceptable Use Policy**. Then click **Create workspace**.

## Step 3: Confirm your WhatsApp number

After you click **Create workspace**, we send a **6-digit code** to your WhatsApp. Enter it in the **"Verify your WhatsApp number"** box.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Wait for the code">
    It usually arrives on WhatsApp in a few seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type it in">
    Enter the six digits, then click **Verify & continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Didn't get it? Resend">
    Wait until the timer ends, then click **Resend**. Don't click it many times. Too many tries make you wait longer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your reseller workspace is then set up in a few seconds. What happens next depends on how you signed up:

| You signed up with | What happens next |
| --- | --- |
| **Email and password** | If the login page opens, [log in](/pa/log-in) with your email and password. Then confirm your email, and pay for your plan. |
| **Google, GitHub, or Facebook** | Your email is already confirmed. The payment screen opens straight away. |

## Step 4: Confirm your email

We send a verification link to your email, and a **"Check your inbox"** screen opens.

<Frame caption="The Check your inbox screen, with three short steps, the Resend verification email button, and a Sign out link">
  <img src="/images/pa/reseller-verify-email.png" width="420" style={{ margin: '0 auto' }} alt="The Check your inbox screen, saying a verification link was sent to your email, with three steps: open the email, click Verify Email Address, and you'll be redirected back, followed by a Resend verification email button, a Sign out link, and a tip to check your spam or junk folder" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the email">
    Find the email from us in your inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Verify Email Address">
    Click the **Verify Email Address** button in the email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go back to your account">
    The link takes you back to your account, and the payment screen opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  No email? Check your spam or junk folder. Or click **Resend verification email** on the screen to get a new link.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Signed up with Google, GitHub, or Facebook? You skip this screen and go straight to payment.
</Note>

## Step 5: Complete your payment

Next, the **"Activate Your Workspace"** screen opens. Pay for your plan in four short parts.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your Selected Plan">
    <Frame caption="The Activate Your Workspace checkout, on step 1 Your Selected Plan, showing the Reseller plan with its monthly price and limits, and the Continue to Billing Details button">
      <img src="/images/pa/reseller-checkout.png" alt="The Activate Your Workspace checkout with four steps on the left, and the Your Selected Plan card showing the Reseller plan, its price per month, its limits, the Total/month, a Change my mind button, and the Continue to Billing Details button" />
    </Frame>

    Check your plan, price, and limits.

    To change the number of client workspaces, click **Change my mind**, move the slider, and click **Done**.

    When everything is right, click **Continue to Billing Details**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Billing Details">
    <Frame caption="The Billing Details step of the checkout, with Country set to India, Gujarat as the state, 380001 as the ZIP code, and the Update & Continue button">
      <img src="/images/pa/reseller-billing-details.png" alt="The Billing Details step of the Activate Your Workspace checkout, with a note that details apply to upcoming invoices only, fields for Company Name, Company Email, GST Number, Country set to India, State / Province Gujarat, ZIP / Postal Code 380001 and Company Address, and the Back and Update & Continue buttons" />
    </Frame>

    Add your company details. They are shown in the **"Bill To"** section of your invoices.

    Fill in **Company Name**, **Company Email**, **GST Number**, **Country**, **State / Province**, **ZIP / Postal Code**, and **Company Address**. **Country** is required.

    <Note>
      These details apply to upcoming invoices only. You can add or change them later.
    </Note>

    Click **Update & Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payment Method">
    Choose **online payment** or **bank transfer**, then click **Continue to Payment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Payment">
    - **Online payment** — click **Pay Now** and finish the payment. Your dashboard opens right away.
    - **Bank transfer** — pay to the bank details shown, upload your payment proof, and click **Submit Payment Proof**. Your dashboard opens after our team approves it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 6: Your reseller account is ready

Once your payment is done, your **reseller dashboard** opens.

<Frame caption="The reseller dashboard, with the Plan, Status, Renewal and Client workspaces cards at the top, the Your customers chart, and Usage & Limits at the bottom">
  <img src="/images/pa/reseller-dashboard.png" alt="The reseller dashboard, with a Good morning greeting and a Refresh button, cards for Plan Reseller billed monthly, Status Active, Renewal and Client workspaces 0, a Your customers section with a Client workspaces added chart and a breakdown by Active, Pending payment, Suspended and Inactive, and a Usage & Limits section with used and total counts for features such as Client workspaces, Automations, Campaigns and Contacts" />
</Frame>

Here you can see:

- **Plan** and **Status** — your own WaMatrix.io plan, and whether it is active.
- **Client workspaces** — how many client workspaces you have, and how many are active.
- **Your customers** — a chart of client workspaces added over the last 12 months, and how many are active, pending payment, suspended, or inactive.
- **Usage & Limits** — for each feature in your plan, how much you have used and your limit, such as **Contacts 0 / 70,000**.
