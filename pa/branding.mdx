## System Core Settings

Go to **System Settings → System**. This sets your platform name and the logos shown across the panel.

<Frame caption="The System Core Settings page, with the site name, locale defaults, and the logo and favicon upload fields">
  <img src="/images/pa/system-core-settings.png" alt="The System Core Settings page, with the site name, locale defaults, and the logo and favicon upload fields" />
</Frame>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Platform details">
    | Field | Description |
    | ----- | ----------- |
    | **Site Name** | Your platform name. Shown across the panel, and used in emails if you do not upload a header logo. |
    | **Site Description** | A short description of your platform. |
    | **Timezone** | The timezone used across your panel, and the default for new tenants. |
    | **Date Format** | How dates are written across the panel, such as `2026-09-16` or `16/09/2026`. |
    | **Time Format** | 12-hour or 24-hour clock. |
    | **Default Country Code** | The country code filled in by default on phone number fields. |

    <Note>
      **Timezone**, **Date Format**, and **Time Format** set the default for your tenants. Each tenant can change them for their own workspace.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Logos & favicon">
    | Field | Recommended size | Maximum |
    | ----- | ---------------- | ------- |
    | **Site Logo** | 200 × 50 px | 2 MB |
    | **Dark Logo** | 200 × 50 px | 2 MB |
    | **Favicon** | 32 × 32 px | 512 KB |

    The **Dark Logo** is used in dark mode. Upload both. One does not fall back to the other, so if you skip the dark logo, no logo shows in dark mode at all.

    Logos must be **JPEG, PNG, or WebP**. The favicon must be **PNG**. SVG files are not accepted for the Site Logo, Dark Logo, or Favicon.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Email Layout

Go to **Settings → Email Layout**. This controls how every email to your tenants looks. A preview updates beside the settings as you change them.

<Frame caption="The Email Layout page on its Branding tab, with the live email preview alongside">
  <img src="/images/pa/email-layout.png" alt="The Email Layout page on its Branding tab, with the live email preview alongside" />
</Frame>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Branding">
    Upload the logo shown at the top of your emails. If you do not upload one, your **Site Name** is used as text instead.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Colours">
    Set the header, content, footer, button, and link colours. Pick a colour, or type a hex value such as `#1A73E8`.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Footer">
    Set the text at the bottom of every email. Two placeholders fill themselves in:

    - `{date_year}` — the current year.
    - `{company_name}` — your company name from [Billing & payouts](/pa/billing-and-payouts#company-information). If you have not set one, your **Site Name** is used.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Click **Save Changes** on the last tab to save all three.

## Sender Identity (SMTP)

Go to **Settings → Email**. SMTP is the connection the platform uses to send email. Until it works, your tenants receive nothing.

Your email provider gives you these details. That could be your hosting company, your Google Workspace account, or a sending service you signed up for.

<Frame caption="The Email Configuration page, with the SMTP connection fields, sender identity, and the Send Test Email box">
  <img src="/images/pa/smtp-settings.png" alt="The Email Configuration page, with the SMTP connection fields, sender identity, and the Send Test Email box" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on Enable Email">
    Use the switch at the top of the card. The fields below are only required once this is on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the SMTP details">
    Enter the **SMTP Host**, **SMTP Port**, **SMTP Username**, **SMTP Password**, and **SMTP Encryption**. Encryption is **TLS**, **SSL**, or **None** — your provider tells you which, and TLS on port 587 is the most common.

    **SMTP Protocol** has one option, **SMTP**. Leave it as it is.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set who the email comes from">
    Enter the **Sender Name** and **Sender Email**. This is what a tenant sees in their inbox, so use your business name and your own address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test email">
    Enter an address in the **Send Test Email** box and send. If it arrives, your setup works. If it does not, check the host, port, encryption, and password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  No email is sent until SMTP works. Set this up before you open signups, or a new tenant will never get their registration email.
</Warning>

## Legal

Go to **Settings → Legal** to add your **Privacy Policy** and **Terms & Conditions** links. These appear when a tenant signs up.

<Frame caption="The Legal settings page, with the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions link fields">
  <img src="/images/pa/legal-settings.png" alt="The Legal settings page, with the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions link fields" />
</Frame>

Turn on the switch at the top of the card, then enter both links:

- **Privacy Policy Link** — the full URL, including `https://`.
- **Terms & Conditions Link** — the full URL, including `https://`.

Once the switch is on, both links are required. Click **Save Changes**.

### Where the links appear

The links show on the tenant signup page, next to the agreement checkbox. The checkbox says _"I agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy."_

<Frame caption="The tenant sign-up page, with Terms of Service and Privacy Policy shown as links beside the agreement checkbox">
  <img src="/images/pa/signup-consent-links.png" alt="The tenant sign-up page, with Terms of Service and Privacy Policy shown as links beside the agreement checkbox" />
</Frame>

A tenant cannot finish registration without ticking it.
