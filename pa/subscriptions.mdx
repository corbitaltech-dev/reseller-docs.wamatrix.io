Every tenant on a paid or trial plan has a subscription. Use this page to check which plan a tenant is on, whether their billing is up to date, and when they renew.

A subscription starts one of three ways: a tenant checks out through a live payment gateway, you approve their bank transfer under [**Offline Payments**](/pa/offline-payments), or they are picked up automatically when a free or trial plan is assigned during [onboarding](/pa/onboarding-a-sub-tenant). Picking a paid plan during onboarding only records the choice — the subscription itself only starts once payment settles.

## What you see on the screen

| Column | Description |
| ------ | ----------- |
| **Tenant** | Name of the tenant. Click the name to view their subscription details. |
| **Plan** | Plan currently assigned to the tenant. |
| **Status** | Current subscription status. |
| **Cycle** | Billing period, such as monthly or yearly. |
| **Paid Via** | How the tenant paid. See [Payment methods](#payment-methods) below. |
| **Renews** | Date when the current billing period ends. |
| **Started** | Date when the subscription started. |

### Filter the list

Use the filter panel to narrow down a long list of tenants:

- **Status** — Trial, Active, or Expired. Billing Only and Suspended are not offered as filter choices yet, even though both appear in the **Status** column. See [Common questions](#common-questions) below.
- **Plan** — show only tenants on one plan.
- **Created From** / **Created To** — a date range for when the subscription started.
- **Expiring Soon** — tenants renewing within 7, 14, or 30 days.
- **Billing Period** — monthly, yearly, and so on.
- **Paid Via** — filter by payment method, including methods you have never actually been paid with yet.
- **Trial Active** — only tenants currently on a trial.
- **Expiring This Week** — a quick toggle for this week's renewals.
- **New This Month** — tenants who started this month.

## Payment methods

The **Paid Via** column and filter show how the tenant is paying:

| Value | It means |
| --- | --- |
| **Razorpay**, **PayPal**, **Tap Payments**, **Paymob** | The tenant paid through a live gateway you configured under [Payment Gateway Settings](/pa/billing-and-payouts#payment-gateway-settings). They checked out directly — you never see or touch the card details. |
| **Bank Transfer** | The tenant paid you directly and you approved their payment request under [Offline Payments](/pa/offline-payments). |
| **Trial** | A free or trial plan, with no payment involved. |

<Note>
  A row can briefly show no payment method at all. This happens right after a tenant picks a paid plan during onboarding but before they finish paying — the pick is recorded, but the subscription itself has not started yet.
</Note>

## Subscription status

| Status | Description |
| ------ | ----------- |
| **Trial** | Tenant is using a free plan's trial period. Trials do not go through grace or billing-only — a lapsed trial expires directly, with no extra days added. |
| **Active** | Subscription is active and the tenant has access to the plan. |
| **Billing Only** | Payment is overdue. The tenant can sign in and make the payment, but other features are unavailable. |
| **Suspended** | Automatic payment retries (dunning) have failed after the allowed attempts, and access is stopped. This is different from manually suspending a tenant — see the note below. |
| **Expired** | The subscription period has ended and the plan was not renewed. |
| **Cancelled** | Not a separate status shown in this column. A cancelled subscription keeps its current status (usually **Active**) until the period ends, then becomes **Expired**. See the note below. |

<Warning>
  **Suspended here is not the same as manually suspending a tenant.** This page's **Suspended** status is set automatically, only when repeated payment retries fail on a tenant with auto-renewal switched on. Manually suspending a tenant from [**Managing tenants → Suspend, deactivate, and reactivate**](/pa/managing-customers#suspend-deactivate-and-reactivate) is a separate action on the tenant's account. A manual suspension does not change anything on this page — this **Status** column can still say **Active** while the tenant is locked out. To check a manual suspension, look at the tenant's own status on the [tenant list](/pa/managing-customers), not here.
</Warning>

### Subscription status flow

The status changes automatically based on the subscription and payment status.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Grace period" icon="clock">
    When the subscription period ends, the tenant enters the **grace period** and keeps full access while payment is pending.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Billing Only" icon="credit-card">
    After the grace period ends, the status changes to **Billing Only**. The tenant can sign in and complete the pending payment, but other features are unavailable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Suspended or Expired" icon="circle-x">
    From **Billing Only**, the subscription goes one of two ways:
    - If automatic payment retries keep failing, the status changes to **Suspended** and access is stopped completely — the tenant can no longer sign in.
    - If the billing-only period ends with no payment at all, the subscription becomes **Expired**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Tenants with **Billing Only** status can still sign in and complete their payment. Their full access is restored after the payment is successful. Tenants with **Suspended** status cannot sign in — the subscription must be manually reactivated or a new plan assigned.
</Info>

You can configure the grace period and billing-only period under [**Settings → Subscription Lifecycle**](/pa/core-platform#grace-period-suspension).

<Note>
  If a tenant cancels their subscription, the **Status** column does not change — it stays whatever it was (usually **Active**) until the current subscription period ends, then becomes **Expired**. Cancellation does not stop access immediately, and there is no separate "Cancelled" badge in the list. A tenant cancels from their own billing page, not from this admin panel. To confirm whether a tenant has cancelled, check the **Timeline** on their subscription details page.
</Note>

## View subscription details

Click a tenant's name to open the subscription details page.

  <img src="/images/pa/subscription-detail.png" alt="A tenant's subscription detail page, with the plan summary, invoices, timeline, and feature list" />

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Plan** | Plan name, price, and billing period. |
| **Timeline** | The dates on record for this subscription. See the table below. |
| **Features** | Features and limits available with the tenant's plan. |
| **Invoices** | Invoices related to the subscription, which can be opened and downloaded as PDF. |

### Timeline events

The **Timeline** only shows events that actually happened on this subscription — a fresh trial, for example, shows only **Started**.

| Event | It means |
| --- | --- |
| **Started** | The subscription was first created. |
| **Current period start** | The date the current billing period began. |
| **Current period end** | The date the current billing period ends, or ended. |
| **Grace period ends** | The last day of full access after the period ended. |
| **Suspended until** | Set only while the subscription is in the automatic **Suspended** state. |
| **Canceled** | The date the tenant cancelled. Access continues until the period ends. |
| **Ends** | The date access actually stops for a cancelled subscription — the same date as **Current period end** at the time of cancelling. |

## Manage subscriptions

The Subscriptions page is mainly used to view subscription information. You cannot change the plan or renewal date from this page. The only row action is **Delete**.

<Note>
  **Delete** only appears for reseller staff with the **Delete Subscriptions** permission. If a team member doesn't see it, check their role under [Users & roles](/pa/users-and-roles).
</Note>

<Warning>
  **Delete permanently removes the subscription.** This action cannot be undone. Use it only when a subscription was created by mistake. Do not use Delete to cancel a tenant's subscription.
</Warning>

### Upgrades and downgrades

Tenants change their own plan from their billing page. You cannot start an upgrade or downgrade for them from this admin panel, but the result shows up here:

- **Upgrading** takes effect right away. If the tenant had time left on their old plan, that time is turned into a proration credit and shown as a line item on their next invoice.
- **Downgrading** never takes effect mid-cycle. The tenant keeps their current plan until the billing period ends — the downgrade only applies at the next renewal.

Both show up as a **Plan upgraded** or **Plan downgraded** event on the tenant's Timeline.

## How it connects

- [**Offline Payments**](/pa/offline-payments) — where you approve or reject a tenant's bank transfer, which is what actually activates or renews a **Bank Transfer** subscription.
- [**Plans & pricing**](/pa/plans-and-pricing) — where you set up the plans, prices, and trial days that show up on this page.
- [**Settings → Subscription Lifecycle**](/pa/core-platform#grace-period-suspension) — where grace period, billing-only days, and dunning retries are configured.
- [**Settings → Subscription Lifecycle → Deletion Scheduling**](/pa/core-platform#deletion-scheduling) — see [Common questions](#common-questions) below for why this matters after a subscription is Suspended.
- [**Managing tenants**](/pa/managing-customers#suspend-deactivate-and-reactivate) — where you manually suspend, deactivate, or delete a tenant, separately from anything on this page.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why does a cancelled subscription still show Active?" icon="circle-help">
    Cancelling only stops renewal. The tenant already paid for the current period, so they keep access, and the **Status** column keeps showing **Active** (or whatever it already was) until that period ends. It then becomes **Expired**. Check the **Timeline** on the tenant's subscription detail page to see the actual cancellation date.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I upgrade or downgrade a tenant's plan from here?" icon="circle-help">
    No. A tenant changes their own plan from their billing page. This page only shows the result, as a **Plan upgraded** or **Plan downgraded** event on their Timeline.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I filter by Suspended or Billing Only status?" icon="circle-help">
    The **Status** filter currently only offers Trial, Active, and Expired, even though **Billing Only** and **Suspended** both appear as real values in the **Status** column. To find suspended or billing-only tenants today, sort by **Renews** or check tenants flagged by dunning payment-failure alerts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens after a subscription is Suspended?" icon="circle-help">
    If nothing changes, the tenant's account moves toward deletion. Suspension through failed payment retries automatically schedules the tenant for deletion after the delay set in [**Deletion Scheduling**](/pa/core-platform#deletion-scheduling) — 14 days by default — unless you have already scheduled a deletion for them yourself. Reactivate the subscription or assign a new plan before that date if you want to keep the tenant.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
