**Setup → Activity Log** records actions performed on your platform, including **who** performed the action, **what** they changed, and **when**.

The log can help you track changes made by staff and tenants.

## Activity details

<Frame caption="The Activity Log, with the description, event, actor and time for each entry">
  <img src="/images/pa/activity-log.png" alt="The Activity Log, with the description, event, actor and time for each entry" />
</Frame>

| Column | Description |
| ------ | ----------- |
| **Description** | What happened. |
| **Event** | Type of action, such as created, updated, or deleted. |
| **Performed By** | User who performed the action. |
| **Subject** | Item affected, such as a plan or tenant. |
| **When** | Date and time of the action. |

<Tip>
  You can search by **Description** or **Event**, and filter by **Event**.
</Tip>

<Note>
  The log includes actions from both your admin panel and tenant workspaces. If an entry shows an unfamiliar user, it may have been created by a tenant's staff member.
</Note>

## Delete activity logs

You can delete logs in three ways.

- **Single entry** — Delete one entry from its row.
- **Multiple entries** — Select entries and click **Delete**.
- **All entries** — Click **Clear all logs**.

<Warning>
  Deleted logs cannot be restored. **Clear all logs** permanently removes the complete activity history.
</Warning>

<Info>
  Give the **Delete** permission only to trusted staff members. Staff with this permission can remove activity records. See [Users & roles](/pa/users-and-roles).
</Info>
