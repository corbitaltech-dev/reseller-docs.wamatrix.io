## Google Accounts

Allow tenants to connect their Google account for features such as **Google Sheets** and **Google Calendar**.

<Warning>
  Google Accounts uses a **separate Google app** from Social Login. Both require different credentials.
</Warning>

<Frame caption="The Google Accounts tab, with the client credentials and the redirect URI to register">
  <img src="/images/pa/google-accounts.png" alt="The Google Accounts tab, with the client credentials and the redirect URI to register" />
</Frame>

### Required settings

| Field | Where it comes from |
| ----- | ------------------- |
| **Client ID** | Your Google Cloud project. |
| **Client Secret** | Your Google Cloud project. |
| **Authorized Redirect URI** | The redirect URL shown on this page. |

<Info>
  Copy the **Authorized Redirect URI** exactly as shown and add it to your Google Cloud project. An incorrect URI can cause a `redirect_uri_mismatch` error when a tenant connects their Google account.
</Info>
