A tenant can join your platform in two ways. They sign up on your registration page, or you create their account from the panel.

You control both. Set everything up here before your first tenant arrives.

<Warning>
  Set up [**registration and email**](#registration-verification-settings), [**bot protection**](#bot-protection), and [**social login**](#social-login) before tenants start registering. These settings apply to new registrations only and do not affect existing tenants.
</Warning>

## Registration & Verification Settings

Go to **Settings → Tenant Settings**.

<Frame caption="The Tenant Settings page, with the registration toggles and the tenant plan limit alert">
  <img src="/images/pa/tenant-settings.png" alt="The Tenant Settings page, with the registration toggles and the tenant plan limit alert" />
</Frame>

### Allow tenant registration

**Enable Registration** lets anyone create their own account on your signup page. It is **on by default**.

Using this setting, you can close your registration page. Nobody new can sign up.
Your existing tenants are not affected. You can also still [add tenants yourself from the panel](#adding-a-tenant).

### Verification and emails

These settings are **off by default**:

| Setting | What it does |
| ------- | ------------ |
| **Enable Email Verification** | Requires tenants to verify their email before signing in. Verification links are valid for 60 minutes and can be sent again. |
| **Enable Registration Success Email** | Sends a confirmation email after registration. |
| **Enable Send Welcome Mail** | Sends a welcome email with the tenant's plan and dashboard link. If email verification is enabled, it is sent after verification. |

<Note>
  Only tenants who register through your signup page need email verification. Tenants you [add from the panel](#adding-a-tenant), and anyone signing in with Google or Facebook, are already verified.

  Set up [**SMTP**](/pa/branding#email-configuration) before you turn this on.
</Note>

## Customer Plan Limit Alerts

**Customer Near Plan Limit** warns a tenant by email when they are close to using up a limit on their plan. It is **off by default**.

The email goes out at **80%** of the limit by default. You can change this to any value from **50%** to **99%**.

<Note>
  This email goes to the tenant, not to you. It is about the plan you sold them.

  For an alert about your **own** WaMatrix.io plan, turn on **Plan Limit Almost Reached** under [**Settings → Notification**](/pa/core-platform#notification).
</Note>

## Bot Protection

Bot protection stops fake signups. It adds a check to your login, password recovery, and registration pages.

Go to **Settings → Infrastructure & Security → Re-Captcha** to turn it on.

<Frame caption="The Bot Protection page, with the reCAPTCHA and Turnstile provider options and their key fields">
  <img src="/images/pa/bot-protection.png" alt="The Bot Protection page, with the reCAPTCHA and Turnstile provider options and their key fields" />
</Frame>

| Provider | Required keys |
| --- | --- |
| **Google reCAPTCHA v3** | Requires a **Site Key** and **Secret Key** from Google. |
| **Cloudflare Turnstile** | Requires a **Site Key** and **Secret Key** from Cloudflare. |

You can use only one provider at a time, and bot protection is **off by default**.

<Warning>
  Wrong keys block tenants from logging in, not just from registering. Check that your keys match the provider you picked before you turn this on.
</Warning>

## Social Login

Social login lets tenants sign in with **Google** or **Facebook**, so they do not need another password. Both are **off by default**.

Go to **Settings → Infrastructure & Security → Social Login** to turn them on.

<Frame caption="The Social Login Settings page, with the Google and Facebook credential and redirect URI fields">
  <img src="/images/pa/social-login.png" alt="The Social Login Settings page, with the Google and Facebook credential and redirect URI fields" />
</Frame>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Google">
    **Required credentials:** Client ID and Client Secret.

    Add the redirect URL shown on this page to your Google developer console as the **Authorized Redirect URI**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Facebook">
    **Required credentials:** App ID and App Secret.

    Add the redirect URL shown on this page to your Facebook developer console as the **Valid OAuth Redirect URI**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

A new tenant signing in with Google or Facebook for the first time gets an account created automatically.

<Info>
  Copy the redirect URL exactly as shown, including `https://`. If it does not match, social login will not work.
</Info>

## Adding a Tenant

There are two ways in. You can create the account yourself from the panel, which is useful for a tenant you sold to directly, or let them register on your signup page.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="From the panel">
    <Frame caption="The Add Tenant form, with the tenant, workspace, plan, and password fields">
      <img src="/images/pa/add-tenant-form.png" alt="The Add Tenant form, with the tenant, workspace, plan, and password fields" />
    </Frame>

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the Add Tenant form">
        Go to **Tenants** and click **Add Tenant**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the tenant details">
        | Field | Description |
        | ----- | ----------- |
        | **Full name** and **Email** | The tenant's name and email address. |
        | **Workspace** | The tenant's business or company name. |
        | **Tenant Name** | Becomes the tenant's subdomain, so it must be different from every other tenant. Filled in from the **Workspace** name, and you can edit it. Lowercase letters and numbers only, with no spaces or hyphens. |
        | **Phone** | The tenant's phone number. **Country** and **Address** are optional. |
        | **Plan** | Optional. Leave **Select a plan** if the plan will be assigned later. |
        | **Password** and **Confirm Password** | Set and confirm the tenant's password. |
      </Step>
      <Step title="Save">
        Click **Save**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Info>
      The platform does not email these details to the tenant. Send their email address and password to them yourself.
    </Info>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Public registration">
    When **Enable Registration** is enabled, tenants can create their own accounts and choose a plan during registration.

    Their workspace is created automatically after registration.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## What the tenant sees

A new tenant picks a plan and pays. What happens next depends on the payment method.

<Steps>
  <Step title="They pay online" icon="credit-card">
    The plan starts straight away. The tenant is redirected to their dashboard and can start using the features their plan includes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or they pay by bank transfer" icon="landmark">
    They see a **Pending Approval** screen until you check the payment. Once you approve it under [**Offline payments**](/pa/offline-payments), the plan starts and their dashboard opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To see what their plan includes, a tenant opens **My Subscription**. That page lists their features and shows how much of each limit they have used.

<Tip>
  Want to see what they see? Go to **Tenant List → Impersonate** to open their account as they would. A **You are impersonating this tenant** banner shows while you are in, and **Stop Impersonating** takes you back.
</Tip>
