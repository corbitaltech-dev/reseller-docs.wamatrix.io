Your platform collects payment in **one** currency. Every plan you price, every invoice you issue, and every payment your tenants make uses it.

When you type `499` into a plan price, your base currency is what decides whether that means 499 rupees or 499 dollars. It also puts the right symbol next to it, so your tenants see `₹499` wherever the price appears.

## Why the base currency matters

This is not just about which symbol your tenants see. Nothing else in billing works until it is set.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="It unlocks plan creation" icon="layers" href="/pa/plans-and-pricing">
    Plans cannot be created until a base currency exists. Every plan is automatically tagged with it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="It defines what your prices mean" icon="tag">
    A price of `499` is meaningless on its own. The base currency is what makes it ₹499 rather than $499.
  </Card>
  <Card title="It sets the symbol everywhere" icon="badge-dollar-sign">
    Your symbol is used on your pricing page, in the panel, on invoices and PDFs, and in billing emails.
  </Card>
  <Card title="It scopes taxes and coupons" icon="percent" href="/pa/taxes">

  </Card>

### Where the currency appears

| Area | How the currency is used |
| ---- | ------------------------ |
| [**Plans**](/pa/plans-and-pricing) | Every plan is priced in the base currency and tagged with it on save. |
| **Your pricing page** | Plan prices are shown to visitors with your symbol. |
| [**Subscriptions**](/pa/subscriptions) | Every renewal charges the tenant in your base currency. |
| [**Invoices**](/pa/invoices) | Invoice totals, line items, and generated PDFs use your symbol. |
| [**Offline payments**](/pa/offline-payments) | Submitted amounts and approval alerts are shown in the base currency. |
| [**Coupons**](/pa/coupons) | Fixed-amount discounts are deducted in the base currency. |
| [**Taxes**](/pa/taxes) | Tax rates are tagged with the base currency. |
| **Billing emails** | Amounts in tenant-facing emails are formatted with your symbol. |

<Warning>
  The platform never converts between currencies. If your base currency is USD and your plan costs `49`, a tenant in India pays $49. They do not see a price in rupees.
</Warning>

## Before you choose

Pick the currency you actually **bank and settle in**. Then check it against the points below.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Your payment gateway must accept it" icon="credit-card">
    Your tenants pay you through the [payment gateways](/pa/billing-and-payouts) you connect. Check that your gateway and merchant account can settle in your chosen currency before you go live.

    If your gateway cannot accept it, no tenant can pay online. You would be left with [offline payments](/pa/offline-payments) only.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="It should match your pricing strategy" icon="layers">
    Your base currency is your pricing currency for every market you sell into. Choose the one most of your tenants will pay in.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="You cannot change the ISO code later" icon="lock">
    The **ISO Code** is the three-letter code for your currency, such as `INR` or `USD`. It is the part that says what your prices actually mean.

    You can change it freely while you are setting up. But the moment your first tenant starts paying, it is locked, and you cannot switch from `INR` to `USD` afterwards.

    The **Currency Name** and **Symbol** stay editable. See [what you can and cannot change later](#what-you-can-and-cannot-change-later).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Set the base currency

Open **Currency** in the sidebar. The page shows a **Base Currency** card with an **Add Currency** button, which disappears once a currency is saved.

<Frame caption="The Edit Currency panel, showing the Currency Name, ISO Code and Symbol fields">
  <img src="/images/pa/currency-edit-sheet.png" alt="The Edit Currency panel, showing the Currency Name, ISO Code and Symbol fields" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add Currency">
    This opens the currency form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the fields">
    | Field | Required | Description |
    | ----- | -------- | ----------- |
    | **Currency Name** | Yes | The full name shown in the panel, for example `Indian Rupee`. Up to 100 characters. |
    | **ISO Code** | Yes | The 3-character ISO 4217 code, for example `INR`. Typed manually and uppercased automatically. |
    | **Symbol** | Yes | The symbol printed next to every amount, for example `₹`. Up to 10 characters. |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. Your currency now shows in the **Base Currency** card. After this, you can create your plans.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Enter the symbol exactly as you want it to appear on invoices and on your pricing page, such as `$`, `₹`, `€`, or `A$`. This is what your tenants see.
</Tip>

## What you can and cannot change later

<Warning>
  Once any tenant has an **active**, **past-due**, or **billing-only** subscription, the **ISO Code is locked**. You cannot change INR to USD.
</Warning>

| Field | Before you have subscriptions | After a subscription is active |
| ----- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| **Currency Name** | Editable | Editable |
| **Symbol** | Editable | Editable |
| **ISO Code** | Editable | **Locked** |
