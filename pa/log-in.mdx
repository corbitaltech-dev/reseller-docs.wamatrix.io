Your reseller workspace is where you manage your branding, plans, and tenants.

<Note>
  Set up your [custom domain](/pa/custom-domain) first. You can log in to your reseller workspace once the domain is **verified**.
</Note>

## Get your login details

Once your custom domain is verified, you get a welcome email with your login details:

- **Workspace** — the login link for your reseller workspace, such as `https://app.yourcompany.com/login`.
- **Sign in with** — the email you log in with.
- **Plan** — your reseller plan.

## Which email and password to use

| How you signed up | Email | Password |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **With email and password** on the sign-up form | The email you entered on the sign-up form. | The password you entered on the sign-up form. |
| **With Google, GitHub, or Facebook** | The email of that Google, GitHub, or Facebook account. | Set one first. Your welcome email has a **Set your password** button. Click it, choose a password, and click **Set password**. |

## Sign in to your reseller workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your login page">
    Go to your own domain with `/login` at the end, such as `https://app.yourcompany.com/login`. Or click the link in your welcome email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email and password">
    Enter the email and password for how you signed up, as shown in [Which email and password to use](#which-email-and-password-to-use).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Log in">
    Your reseller workspace opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>
