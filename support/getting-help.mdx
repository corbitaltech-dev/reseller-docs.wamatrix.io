WaMatrix Reseller support is async-first — email and chat over phone calls. Response times are measured in hours, not days. Founding Resellers also get a direct line to the founder on WhatsApp.

## Support Channels

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Priority Email & Chat" icon="envelope">
    Submit a ticket or chat from within your Reseller Dashboard. Priority queue for all reseller accounts — target response time under 4 hours during business hours.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Founder on WhatsApp" icon="message">
    Available to Founding Resellers only. You receive the founder's WhatsApp number on your onboarding call. Best for urgent issues or quick product questions.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What to Include in a Support Request

When reaching out, include the following to help the team respond as quickly as possible:

- Your reseller account email
- The affected workspace name (if the issue is client-related)
- A description of the issue with steps to reproduce it
- Screenshots or screen recordings where relevant
- The full error message text (if applicable)

## Response Time Expectations

| Channel | Expected Response | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Priority Chat | Under 2 hours (business hours) | In-dashboard chat widget |
| Priority Email | Under 4 hours (business hours) | support@wamatrix.io |
| Founder WhatsApp | Within hours, async | Founding Resellers only |

## Your Clients' Support is Your Responsibility

<Note>
  WaMatrix supports you as the reseller. Your clients contact you for support — not WaMatrix. This keeps the white-label experience intact. WaMatrix backs you up async when you escalate.
</Note>
