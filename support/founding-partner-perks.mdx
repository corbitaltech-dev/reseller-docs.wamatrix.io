WaMatrix Reseller opened with 50 founding partner spots. Founding Resellers get a set of permanent benefits that are not available to resellers who join after all spots fill — most importantly, a lifetime lock on their launch pricing.

## Lifetime Price Lock Explained

The price you pay when you sign up as a Founding Reseller is the price you pay forever. It does not increase when WaMatrix raises standard pricing, when you add more client workspaces, or when new features are added to the platform. This guarantee applies as long as your subscription remains active — with no lapse.

## How to Activate Your Perks

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Submit your application at [wamatrix.io/reseller](https://wamatrix.io/reseller).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get Accepted">
    The team reviews your application and replies within 72 hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the Onboarding Call">
    Join the onboarding call with the founder to get set up and aligned.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Perks Go Live">
    Your founding benefits are active from day one of your subscription — no extra steps required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive the Founder's WhatsApp Number">
    You'll receive the founder's direct WhatsApp number at the end of your onboarding call.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Founding partner benefits are tied to continuous subscription. If your subscription lapses and you re-subscribe, standard pricing at the time of re-subscription applies. The lifetime price lock does not survive a subscription cancellation.
</Warning>

## After the 50 Spots Fill

Once all 50 founding spots are claimed, the founding partner program closes permanently. New resellers who join after that point do so at standard pricing and without access to any of the founding perks. The live spot count is updated in real time on the [wamatrix.io/reseller](https://wamatrix.io/reseller) page — check there to see how many spots remain.
