WaMatrix Reseller access is application-based — it is not a self-serve sign-up. Every application is reviewed personally by the WaMatrix team, and not every applicant is accepted. This is intentional: keeping the partner network selective ensures a higher quality of support, faster feature iteration based on real agency needs, and a reseller community worth being part of.

## The Application Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fill out the application">
    Visit [wamatrix.io/reseller](https://wamatrix.io/reseller) and complete the short application form. It takes approximately 5 minutes. You'll be asked about your agency, the types of clients you work with, and how you plan to use the platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Application review">
    The WaMatrix team reviews every application personally. Expect a reply within **72 hours**. If your application is shortlisted, you may be asked a follow-up question or two before a final decision is made.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Founder onboarding call">
    If accepted, you'll receive a personal onboarding call with the WaMatrix founder. This is a real conversation — not a sales call. The goal is to make sure you're set up for success and that the platform is a genuine fit for your use case.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dashboard access">
    After the onboarding call, you gain access to your master reseller dashboard. From there, you can connect your custom domain, configure your branding, and start creating client workspaces immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Founding Reseller Program

WaMatrix Reseller launched with a founding partner program — **50 spots only**. Once all 50 seats are filled, the founding program closes permanently and standard pricing and terms apply to all future applicants. The page at wamatrix.io/reseller will go offline when the last spot is claimed.

Founding Resellers receive five exclusive benefits that are not available at standard pricing:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Lifetime Price Lock" icon="lock">
    Your monthly reseller fee is locked at the founding rate — forever. Standard pricing increases never apply to your account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Founder on WhatsApp" icon="message">
    Direct WhatsApp access to the WaMatrix founder during your first week and for urgent issues going forward. Real humans, real-time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Priority Feature Requests" icon="star">
    Your feature requests go to the top of the roadmap. As a founding partner, your feedback actively shapes the platform.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Founding Partner Badge" icon="award">
    A permanent founding partner designation on your account — a mark of being among the first 50 resellers on the platform.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Screen-Share Onboarding" icon="desktop">
    A live, screen-share onboarding session to walk through your full setup — domain, branding, first workspace, and plans — with a WaMatrix team member.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

For a full breakdown of every founding reseller benefit, see [Founding Partner Perks](/support/founding-partner-perks).

## What We Look For

WaMatrix Reseller is built for a specific type of partner. Strong applicants typically fall into one of these profiles:

- **Agencies running WhatsApp marketing for multiple clients** — you're already managing WhatsApp campaigns or customer communications on behalf of businesses and want a branded platform to do it at scale.
- **Digital marketing agencies building a recurring SaaS revenue stream** — you want to add a productized WhatsApp service to your offering and keep the margin under your own brand.
- **Developers adding WhatsApp to client projects** — you're building out CRM or communication tools for clients and want a white-labeled WhatsApp backend without building from scratch.

If you're a single business running your own WhatsApp marketing (not on behalf of clients), the [Professional Plan](/introduction#reseller-vs-professional-plan) is likely a better fit.

<Warning>
  Once all 50 founding reseller spots are filled, founding-tier benefits and pricing are **permanently closed** to new applicants. There are no exceptions and no waitlist for founding terms. Apply early if founding access matters to you.
</Warning>
