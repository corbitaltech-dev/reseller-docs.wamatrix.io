WaMatrix Reseller is a platform that lets agencies and consultants launch their own fully branded WhatsApp CRM SaaS — without building anything from scratch. Your customers log in at your own domain (e.g. `app.youragency.com`), see your logo and brand colors, and interact entirely with your product. The WaMatrix name never appears. Behind the scenes, WaMatrix (by Corbital Technologies) runs every layer of infrastructure — servers, uptime, updates, and Meta API compliance — so you can focus entirely on growing your client base and keeping the margin.

## How the Model Works

As a reseller, you pay one flat monthly fee to WaMatrix. That fee covers the full platform, all infrastructure, and all client workspaces you create — regardless of how many clients you onboard. There are no per-seat or per-workspace overage charges built into the reseller fee.

Meta conversation charges work differently: each client connects their own WhatsApp Business account and is billed directly by Meta for any conversation fees they incur. WaMatrix applies **0% markup** to Meta charges — you never handle Meta billing, and your clients are never charged through you for messaging costs. This keeps your billing model clean and your liability minimal.

## What Your Clients Get

Every client workspace includes the full WaMatrix feature set:

- **Bulk broadcast campaigns** — send targeted WhatsApp messages to segmented contact lists
- **WhatsApp chatbot builder** — visual drag-and-drop flow builder, no code required
- **Team shared inbox** — multiple agents handle conversations in a single unified inbox
- **Automation flows** — trigger messages and actions based on contact behavior or events
- **Contact management** — import, tag, segment, and manage contacts at scale
- **Campaign analytics** — open rates, reply rates, and delivery reports per campaign
- **Template management** — create, submit, and manage WhatsApp message templates
- **AI-assisted messaging** — AI suggestions and reply drafts to speed up agent responses

## Reseller vs Professional Plan

WaMatrix offers two primary plan types, and it's worth understanding the difference before you apply.

The **Professional Plan** is designed for a single business running its own WhatsApp marketing — one workspace, one brand, one team. It has no white-label controls and is not intended for managing multiple clients.

The **Reseller Plan** is for agencies managing multiple clients. It includes the master reseller dashboard, white-label domain and branding controls, unlimited sub-workspaces, and custom plan configuration. If you're building a SaaS business or managing WhatsApp marketing on behalf of clients, Reseller is the right plan.
