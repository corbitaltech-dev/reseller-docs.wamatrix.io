Broadcast campaigns let your clients send bulk WhatsApp messages to their contact lists using Meta-approved message templates. Every message is delivered through the official WhatsApp Business Cloud API.

## How Broadcasts Work

When a client runs a broadcast, they pick an approved message template, select a contact segment or list, and optionally personalize dynamic variables (such as the recipient's name or a custom offer). They can send immediately or schedule for a future date and time. WhatsApp delivers each message individually — recipients see the message as a personal conversation, not a group chat, which drives significantly higher engagement than traditional SMS or email blasts.

## Creating a Campaign

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Campaigns module">
    Go to **Campaigns → New Campaign**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the campaign">
    Choose a campaign name for internal tracking (e.g. "July Promo — VIP Customers"). This name is not visible to recipients.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a WhatsApp channel">
    Pick the connected WhatsApp number you want to send from. If your client has multiple numbers, make sure you select the right one for the audience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a message template">
    Choose an approved message template from the list. Preview how it looks with your variables filled in before continuing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select recipients">
    Choose the recipient list — pick an entire contact list, a saved segment, or upload a CSV for a one-time send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map template variables">
    (Optional) Map template variables to contact fields — for example, map `{{1}}` → First Name so each recipient gets a personalized greeting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set send time">
    Choose to **Send Now** or **Schedule** the campaign for a specific date and time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch">
    Review the summary — recipient count, selected template, and sending channel — then click **Launch Campaign**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Campaign Analytics

Once a campaign is sent, the analytics dashboard shows real-time delivery data for every message:

| Metric | Description |
|---|---|
| **Sent** | Total messages dispatched to WhatsApp's API |
| **Delivered** | Messages confirmed as delivered to the recipient's device |
| **Read** | Messages opened by the recipient (requires read receipts to be enabled on their device) |
| **Failed** | Messages that could not be delivered, with a per-record error reason |

Percentages are shown alongside each count so clients can benchmark performance across campaigns. Failed messages display an error reason — common causes include the recipient's number not being registered on WhatsApp, or the contact having opted out of messages.

## Contact Segmentation

Clients can build targeted recipient lists by segmenting contacts using:

- **Custom fields** — e.g. country, plan tier, product purchased
- **Tags** — e.g. "vip", "lead", "newsletter"
- **Last message date** — target contacts who haven't been messaged in X days
- **Campaign engagement** — target contacts who opened (or did not open) a previous campaign

Segments are **dynamic** — they update automatically as contacts match or no longer match the defined criteria. A segment created today will include the right contacts when the campaign runs tomorrow.

<Warning>
  WhatsApp only allows broadcast campaigns to contacts who have **opted in** to receive messages. Sending to non-opted-in contacts violates Meta's Business Messaging Policy and can result in the WhatsApp number being restricted or permanently banned. Always verify opt-in status before launching a campaign.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Schedule campaigns for mid-morning in your client's audience timezone for the highest read rates. Messages sent between 9 AM and 11 AM local time consistently outperform late-night or early-morning sends.
</Tip>
